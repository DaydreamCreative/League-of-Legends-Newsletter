## CHAMPIONS & FINISHES — PRE-SLEEPER (authoritative)

### 2016 — Champion: Elijah Cohen (12-1)
- Runner-up: Austin Hermann (8-5). Also: Drew Knight 3rd (7-6).
- The founding king. Elijah steamrolled the inaugural season and set the first
  record book (see Hall of Records).

### 2017 — Champion: Luke Knight (11-2) [former member]
- Runner-up: Matt Waters (9-4).
- The all-timer asterisk: Zach Smith went **12-1** — the best record in the
  league — and finished **4th**. Best regular season, no ring. Still haunts.

### 2018 — no season (gap year)
- The league took 2018 off — everyone had just graduated college. There simply
  was no 2018 season, so don't treat the missing row as lost data or reference it
  as a mystery.

### 2019 — Champion: Matt Waters (12-1)
- Runner-up: Erica Hallam (9-4); 3rd: Tim Gravely (9-4).
- Matt's first title and a monster year (league-record 205.18 in Week 15).

### 2020 — Champion: John Vaughan (8-5)
- Runner-up: John Sagherian (9-4); 3rd: Matt Waters (11-2).
- THE BATTLE OF THE JOHNS: John V won the title at just 8-5 while John S went
  9-4 and Matt Waters posted the best record (11-2) and *still* didn't win.

### 2021 — Champion: Matt Waters (9-5)
- Runner-up: Tim Gravely (8-6); Ryan Bartlett had the best record (10-4, 4th).
- Matt's second ring; the dynasty talk begins.

> Sleeper-era champions (for color; Sleeper is source of truth for these years):
> 2022 Drew Knight, 2023 Matt Waters. (2022 champ roster below.)
