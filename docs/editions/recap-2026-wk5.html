Week Five: The One Where My Own Column Betrays Me

I sit down every Tuesday to write this newsletter with a steady hand and an honest heart, and most weeks that honesty costs me nothing. This week it costs me plenty, because I have to stand here, in my own publication, and tell you that Straight up Yorkin' it dropped a nearly 120 point outing and still lost. That is not a typo. That is not me being generous to Erica. That is just what happened, and I am contractually and morally obligated to report it even as it pains my soul. Elsewhere, Hampton Water kept its perfect season alive, Matt's moms name turned in the single most lopsided blowout in the history of that matchup, and a trio of upsets scrambled the middle of these standings like eggs at a Sunday brunch nobody asked for.

The Recaps

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) 120.7, Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) 119.92

Let the record show I did everything right. Puka Nacua went off for 29.2, Stafford threw for 27.32, Kyren Williams chipped in 16.6, and my worst bench piece was a washed Dohnte Meyers at 2.2 points, meaning I left basically nothing on the table. I coached a clean game. I lost anyway by less than a point, because Jaylen Warren decided to have the game of his life for the other team, erupting for 33.8 points out of the Erica backfield. That is the fantasy equivalent of getting mugged by a guy who apologizes the whole time. Erica's Deshaun Watson gave her almost nothing at 9.2, but it did not matter with Warren and James Cook (20.3) doing the heavy lifting. I tip my cap through gritted teeth. The series still favors me four to two, and I will be bringing this up again in roughly one year.

JS N My Pants (Dallas) 121.2, Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) 102.84

Isaiah Likely, who Dallas started at a second tight end spot alongside Sam LaPorta, dropped 26.9 points on the league, and Joe Burrow added 24.6 through the air, which is the kind of two headed monster that wins you weeks. Ryan got a monster game out of Bucky Irving, 30.5 points worth, and a strong 20.3 from Jaxon Smith-Njigba, but the rest of his lineup fell apart around them, with Dak Prescott managing just 12.64 and his running back room outside Irving combining for single digits. Dallas's bench was mercifully quiet, nothing scary left unused there, while Ryan watches his record sink to one and four with the ghost of Zach Smith lingering nearby, because this is exactly the kind of team that looks better than its record.

Hampton Water (Austin) 126.56, High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) 115.52

Drake Maye torched the league for 39.46 points, the kind of quarterback week that just ends arguments before they start, and Christian Watson added 29.3 on top of it. Austin is five and oh now and frankly should be mailing the rest of the league an apology letter. Matt R did not go quietly, Jared Goff put up 27.94 and Malik Nabers chipped in 23.6, but he made the mistake of sitting Trevor Lawrence, whose 19.74 points on the bench would have made this a dogfight, and left Emeka Egbuka's 14.2 on the shelf too. Those two alone outscored several of his starters. Hampton Water has now won this long running series three to three, and the Water is apparently fine.

Will the Thrill (Tim) 90.9, How bout them Cowboys (John S) 84.56

Jahmyr Gibbs went for a ridiculous 35.2 and Josh Allen added 30.5, and that duo alone very nearly outscored John S's entire roster by itself. Everything else around them was quiet, Deebo Samuel managed half a point and Jeremiyah Love only 2.2, but Tim did not need much else. John S got a surprise 27.4 out of Parker Washington, who was clearly the headline of his lineup, but Amon-Ra St. Brown was oddly silent at 7.9 and Alvin Kamara gave him almost nothing at 1.6. John S also had Justice Hill sitting on his bench with 8.8 points, which would not have flipped this one but sure would have made it closer. Tim climbs to two and three with the win, John S falls to two and three with a three spot tumble in the standings.

Matt's moms name (Max) 99.94, It might be #over (Drew) 39.82

This one was not a football game so much as a public execution. Max got 19.94 from C.J. Stroud, 17.1 from Trey McBride, and a balanced attack across the board, while Drew's roster simply did not show up, Bo Nix managed 5.92, Brian Thomas posted a flat zero, and his best player on the night, Justin Jefferson, could only scratch out 14.8 points against the tide. Drew did leave Keenan Allen's 7 points on his bench, which, sure, would not have mattered against a near 40 point margin, the widest in the history of this particular matchup according to the record book. Max now leads this head to head series four and oh and some day, somewhere, Drew is going to have nightmares about Matt's moms name.

Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) 115.58, I still own you (John V) 104.3

Garrett Wilson went for 19.9 and Jameson Williams added 17.7, and Jacoby Brissett quietly posted 24.78 at quarterback, enough to get Elijah home. John V got a strong 22 points from Aaron Rodgers and 18 from Brock Bowers, but he was holding Jordan Love's 24.28 on his bench behind Rodgers, a decision that in hindsight looks like leaving a loaded gun in the safe. Elijah improves to four and one and climbs toward the top of the standings, while John V slides to two and three and continues to live out the franchise's eternal theme of a championship ring sitting next to a losing record.

Standings and Movers

Hampton Water sits alone at five and oh, undefeated and insufferable about it. Sampson & Dylan-lila and JS N My Pants both sit at four and one right behind, with Matt's moms name at three and two rounding out the top tier. My own Straight up Yorkin' it squad is stuck at two and three despite a points total, 512.88, that is higher than half the teams above me in the standings, which is either bad luck or a conspiracy and I have not ruled out the latter.

The biggest riser storyline belongs to Serving Punt, up two spots into fifth after that heist against me, proof that stealing from Matt Waters apparently pays well. Will the Thrill also jumped two spots behind the Gibbs explosion. On the other side, How bout them Cowboys cratered three spots down to eighth after that Parker Washington fuelled near miss, and It might be #over dropped two more spots into the basement after that 39.82 point no show, a score so low it belongs in the record book next to Dallas's infamous 34.56 from 2019.

Looking Ahead

Week six brings me a date with High Risk, High Reward, who I intend to treat with the same hospitality Hampton Water showed him this week. Elsewhere, Hampton Water gets to keep its unbeaten streak alive against a struggling Drew squad, the Battle adjacent energy of How bout them Cowboys versus Sampson & Dylan-lila should be a fun litmus test for Elijah's hot streak, and Will the Thrill against JS N My Pants pits two teams trending in opposite directions.

The Back Page

I want it on the record, in ink, forever, that I scored 119.92 points this week and lost. That is not a rebuilding performance. That is a championship caliber outing that ran into a buzzsaw named Jaylen Warren having the game of his life at the worst possible time for me. I remain, despite the record, the most talented two and three team in recorded history, and I would like my bias duly noted and then promptly ignored because I am still right. See everyone next week, when I expect the football gods to apologize.
