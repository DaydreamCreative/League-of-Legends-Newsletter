Week Six: The Unbeaten, The Unhinged, and One Reporter's Extremely Fair Coverage of His Own Team

Gather round, degenerates. Hampton Water sits at five and zero, which in this league is basically a unicorn sighting, and the rest of us are left squinting at the standings wondering how Austin Hermann, who has been chasing a title since the 2016 runner up plaque went on his wall, finally built a juggernaut. Meanwhile I am 2 and 3 with Straight up Yorkin' it, which I will remind you is a temporary and almost certainly misleading data point for a three time champion, because the record book doesn't care about October, it cares about who's hoisting things in December. Six games on the slate, nobody's played a snap yet, so let's get into who's set up to win and who has already, in my professional opinion, written their own eulogy by filling out their lineup card wrong.

The Matchups

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs High Risk, High Reward (Matt R)

I lead this series 2 to 1, and I intend to keep it that way, thank you very much. Matt R comes in at 1 and 4, a record that frankly undersells a roster with Ashton Jeanty and Bucky Irving both in the backfield, which is more firepower at one position than most of this league deserves. But I'm starting Puka Nacua and Davante Adams at wide receiver while Tyreek Hill rots on my bench, and look, I see it, I know it looks insane, but I am a man of conviction and Tyreek has been quiet enough lately that I'm comfortable rolling with the hot hands. Matt R meanwhile is trotting out Rhamondre Stevenson as a flex over Ollie Gordon, which feels like driving a Buick when there's a sports car in the garage, but I'll allow it's not an egregious crime. This is a get right game for both 2 and 3 ish teams and I'm taking myself, because the record book says I do this to Matt R two times out of three and the record book is undefeated.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) vs Serving Punt ✨ (Erica)

Ryan trails this series 1 to 3 and sits at 1 and 4, which for a guy who once dropped 186.92 in a single week back in 2021 feels like a crime against the sport. The widest margin in this series was a gruesome 46.6 blowout back in 2023 week 6, so there's history of this one getting ugly fast. Ryan has Christian McCaffrey and Patrick Mahomes going for him, which should be enough, but benching A.J. Brown for Michael Wilson is the kind of decision that makes a beat reporter sit up straight. Brown is a proven weekly target hog and Wilson is a nice story, not a lineup lock. Erica counters at 3 and 2 with Rashee Rice and Drake London both starting, a genuinely stacked receiver room, plus James Cook and Jaylen Warren in the backfield. I like Erica here, both on talent and on the fact that Ryan's lineup card has me a little worried.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs It might be #over (Drew)

Austin is 5 and 0. Five. Zero. The man has not lost. Drew, on the other hand, is 1 and 4 and his team name is doing some real heavy lifting at this point. Austin leads this head to head 2 to 1 with a 35.74 blowout on the books from 2023, and I see nothing in these lineups that suggests Drew flips the script. Derrick Henry and Omarion Hampton give Austin a two headed monster at running back, and George Kittle at tight end is nearly an unfair advantage most weeks. Drew does have Lamar Jackson slinging it and Justin Jefferson sitting on his bench, which, Drew, my guy, Justin Jefferson should probably not be on your bench ever, under any circumstances, against anyone, but especially not against the only undefeated team in the league. Hampton Water rolls here.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) vs Sampson & Dylan lila (Elijah)

Elijah's team trails this all time series 1 to 2 despite currently sitting at a sparkling 4 and 1, which tells you how long some of these grudges have been cooking. Elijah is the fallen founding king trying to climb back to 2016 glory, and this is a nice measuring stick game against a John S squad that's treading water at 2 and 3. Elijah has Jonathan Taylor and CeeDee Lamb going, which is about as plug and play as fantasy football gets, though I'll note Jalen Hurts is parked on his bench behind Jacoby Brissett, which is a choice, Elijah, a real choice. John S counters with Alvin Kamara and Ladd McConkey, solid, unspectacular, the fantasy equivalent of a sensible sedan. Amon Ra St. Brown riding the bench for John S while Dontayvion Wicks starts is the kind of thing that keeps a man up at night. I like Elijah's talent gap enough to pick him even accounting for the Hurts eyebrow raiser.

Matt's moms name (Max) vs I still own you (John V)

This series is deadlocked at 2 and 2, so no home field edge for anybody, just two teams hovering around five hundred fighting for positioning. Max sits at 3 and 2 with Jayden Daniels and Bijan Robinson anchoring things, a lineup that looks clean top to bottom, no real complaints from the desk here. John V is 2 and 3 and reminding everyone that he won a title in 2020 at 8 and 5 and somehow still owns the worst all time win percentage among active managers, a genuinely baffling bit of career arc. He's started Jordan Love and Quinshon Judkins, fine enough, but benching Ja'Marr Chase for Josh Downs is the sort of thing that makes me want to call John V personally and ask if everything is okay at home, football wise. I'll take Max here, both on lineup construction and on the fact that "I still own you" against Max specifically has not been a true statement in a while.

Will the Thrill (Tim) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

Dallas leads this series a lopsided 4 to 1, including a 33.4 point beatdown in 2024, and Tim walks in here at 2 and 3 against a Dallas squad rolling at 4 and 1. Tim's Mr. Consistent reputation is built on being a perennial lurker, not a puncher, and this lineup does him no favors. Jahmyr Gibbs is sitting on his bench. I'll say that again for the people in the back: Jahmyr Gibbs, a top shelf running back, is benched in favor of Tony Pollard and Jeremiyah Love. That's not a questionable call, that's a lineup emergency, and I'm frankly stunned nobody in his group chat talked him off this ledge. Dallas, meanwhile, has Saquon Barkley and Tetairoa McMillan rolling and no glaring errors that I can find. Dallas wins this one walking, and honestly should.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Hampton Water over It might be #over. Austin is 5 and 0 for a reason and Drew benching Justin Jefferson is not the kind of thing that fixes a losing streak.
  • Upset special: How bout them Cowboys over Sampson & Dylan lila. Elijah's Hurts snub on the bench is exactly the kind of crack a desperate John S squad could slip through.
  • Closest game to call: Straight up Yorkin' it versus High Risk, High Reward. I'm obviously picking myself, but I'll admit under oath that this one could go either way, mostly because of my own suspicious wide receiver decisions.

The Back Page

Bold prediction: Hampton Water keeps rolling to six and zero and officially enters "is anyone allowed to beat this guy" territory, while I drag Straight up Yorkin' it back over five hundred this very week, because a three time champion does not stay 2 and 3 for long, the dynasty is merely resting, and frankly the league should be thanking me for the suspense.
