Week 8: The Thursday Dispatch, Where Hope Still Costs Nothing

Here is the beautiful lie every manager tells themselves on a Wednesday night: this is the week it turns around. Eleven of you believe it right now, reading this with your lineup locked and your coffee getting cold. I believe it too, shamelessly, about my own team, because at 3 and 4 I have exactly the kind of record a dynasty needs for a proper redemption chapter. But before kickoff Thursday, let's survey the carnage that's coming. Elijah's juggernaut sits alone at 6 and 1 looking untouchable, Dallas is quietly piling up points like he's building a bunker, and down in the basement there's a four way tie of desperate men at 2 and 5 who all play each other this year whether they like it or not. Let's get into the matchups.

The Matchups

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs It might be #over (Drew)

I want to be professional about this. I will try. My team, the historically dominant Straight up Yorkin' it, owns this series four games to one, with a 59.6 point beatdown back in 2023 that Drew still has not fully recovered from emotionally. This week I am trotting out Puka Nacua, A.J. Brown, and Ja'Kobi Lane across my receiver slots while leaving Tyreek Hill and Davante Adams on my bench, which is the kind of problem every manager wishes they had, and no, I will not be taking questions about it. Drew counters with Lamar Jackson and a receiving corps led by Justin Jefferson, but I have real concerns about starting Jalen Coker and Caleb Douglas on the perimeter while Keenan Allen and Deebo Samuel Sr. rot on his bench. That is not a winning formula against anybody, let alone against me. Pick: Straight up Yorkin' it, and I genuinely do not feel bad about it.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) vs Hampton Water (Austin)

Ryan leads this series three games to one with a 14.1 point margin back in 2024, but records from the past don't fix a 2 and 5 record in the present, and benching Christian McCaffrey and Malik Nabers while starting Michael Wilson and Antonio Williams at receiver is the kind of lineup decision that keeps a beat reporter employed. Caleb Williams and D'Andre Swift are fine pieces, but Ryan is leaving serious talent on the bench while Austin, sitting pretty at 5 and 2, rolls out Derrick Henry, Jaylen Waddle, and George Kittle without a single head scratcher in the bunch. Austin even has Omarion Hampton stashed behind Woody Marks and RJ Harvey, which is depth most of this league would kill for. Pick: Hampton Water, comfortably.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah)

Elijah is the best team in the league right now at 6 and 1, and he's sent Jacoby Brissett out to throw to CeeDee Lamb and Garrett Wilson, which tells you he's confident enough in his roster to punt the quarterback position and still feel good about it. Erica, for her part, is starting Malik Willis, and I have to ask, out loud, in print, why Justin Herbert is sitting on her bench behind him. That is not a small discrepancy. Erica trails this series one game to three with a brutal 33.2 point loss on the books from 2024, and unless Malik Willis has been secretly working with a quarterback guru this bye week, I don't love her chances of flipping the script. Pick: Sampson & Dylan-lila keeps rolling.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) vs I still own you (John V)

The Battle of the Johns, reborn, as it always is, like some recurring cosmic event. John S leads the all time series three games to one with a double digit margin win back in 2024, but he's 2 and 5 this year and needs this one badly. His lineup actually looks sturdy, Dak Prescott and Amon-Ra St. Brown headline a group with no glaring mistakes. John V counters at 4 and 3 with Jordan Love slinging it to Ja'Marr Chase and Brock Bowers, and I'll note he's got Aaron Rodgers rotting on his bench as the ultimate break glass in case of emergency option. Both lineups are set about as well as they can be. This is the Johns doing what they do, grinding it out with no excuses available. Pick: I still own you, narrowly, riding the better record.

Matt's moms name (Max) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

Max is 0 and 3 all time in this series with a 51.86 point shellacking in the history books from 2023, and Dallas arrives at 5 and 2 looking like a top three team because he is one. Dallas starts Joe Burrow, Saquon Barkley, and a four man receiver room anchored by Tetairoa McMillan and Tee Higgins, with Mike Evans curiously benched behind KC Concepcion Jr., a decision I'll allow since rookies are having a moment this year. Max has Jayden Daniels and Bijan Robinson pulling weight, but keeping Nico Collins and C.J. Stroud glued to his bench while starting Kyle Monangai at flex feels like a swing for the fences he doesn't need to take. Pick: JS N My Pants extends the sweep.

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) vs Will the Thrill (Tim)

Matt R leads this series two games to one, including a 53.96 point massacre in 2023, and both of these teams are languishing below five hundred, which makes this one of those must watch desperation bowls. Matt R starts Trevor Lawrence and a crowded backfield of Bucky Irving, Ollie Gordon II, Rhamondre Stevenson, and Blake Corum, which is more bodies than touches to go around, but nothing egregious. Tim leans on Josh Allen, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Travis Kelce, while stashing Xavier Worthy and Marvin Harrison Jr. on his bench, a luxury most 2 and 5 teams don't get to have. Pick: High Risk, High Reward keeps the series edge intact.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Sampson & Dylan-lila over Serving Punt. Elijah is 6 and 1 for a reason, and Erica's quarterback situation needs an intervention, not a miracle.
  • Upset special: Hampton Water over Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️, mostly because Ryan's bench (McCaffrey! Nabers!) is better than his starting lineup, and that kind of self sabotage has a way of compounding.
  • Too close to call: How bout them Cowboys versus I still own you. Two well built rosters, one ancient Johns rivalry, zero margin for error.

The Back Page

Bold prediction, filed under things I would bet my own trophy case on: Straight up Yorkin' it climbs back to the heart of this race by winning out through Week 10, A.J. Brown and Puka Nacua remind this league why the three time champion never stays down for long, and somewhere Drew Knight starts seriously reconsidering his bench construction while it's still only Week 8. The dynasty isn't dead. It was just resting.
