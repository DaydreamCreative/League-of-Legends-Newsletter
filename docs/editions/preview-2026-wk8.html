Week Eight: The Thursday Night Sermon Before the Sunday Confessional

Gather round, degenerates, because Week Eight is shaping up to be the week the league finally separates the contenders from the pretenders, except for my team, which remains gloriously, stubbornly un-classifiable at two and four. We've got the Battle of the Johns reborn, a 0 and 3 series getting its last gasp of dignity, and a slate where three undefeated adjacent juggernauts, Elijah, Dallas, and Austin, all sit at five and one like they know something the rest of us don't. Somebody's got to lose eventually. Let's see who volunteers.

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs It might be #over (Drew)

I've got the pleasure of covering my own team this week, which I will do with the journalistic integrity you've all come to expect, meaning none. I lead this series four games to one, with a 59.6 point beatdown in 2023 still rattling around Drew's head rent free. My lineup is fine, mostly, though I'll admit watching Roschon Johnson and Javonte Williams both get the start over a bench containing Travis Etienne Jr. and Tyreek Hill is the kind of thing that makes a man question his own objectivity, and I am that man. Puka Nacua and Kyren Williams need to carry me like they're my only two friends in the building. On the other side, Drew is trotting out Lamar Jackson and a receiving corps led by Justin Jefferson, which is respectable, but starting Chris Godwin Jr. and Jalen Coker while Deebo Samuel Sr. rides pine feels like a man who hasn't fully committed to the bit. My pick, and I want it on record that I am shocked, shocked, to find myself picking myself: Straight up Yorkin' it.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) vs Hampton Water (Austin)

Ryan owns this series three games to one with a 14.1 point margin back in 2024, but history means nothing against a team rolling at five and one like Austin's Hampton Water. Ryan's got Christian McCaffrey and Malik Nabers anchoring things, plus Caleb Williams slinging it, and I have to ask why Patrick Mahomes is parked on the bench behind Caleb Williams, a question Ryan will not enjoy answering publicly. Austin counters with Derrick Henry, Jaylen Waddle, and George Kittle, a lineup that looks like it was built by someone who actually read his depth charts, though starting Woody Marks and RJ Harvey together while Omarion Hampton sits is a little curious given the touches Hampton's been fighting for. Still, five and one is five and one. I'll take Hampton Water to keep the record gaudy.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah)

Elijah sits atop the entire league at five and one and owns this series three games to one, including a 33.2 point blowout in 2024 that Erica would like stricken from the record. Elijah's got Jonathan Taylor and Ashton Jeanty in the same backfield, which is simply unfair, plus CeeDee Lamb and Garrett Wilson out wide. That said, benching Jalen Hurts for Jared Goff is a choice I have thoughts about, though Goff has been steady enough not to panic. Erica's got Deshaun Watson at the helm with Rashee Rice and Drake London, a serviceable if unspectacular group, and I'd gently note Justin Herbert sitting behind Watson on her bench is the kind of depth chart decision that keeps columnists like me employed. The fallen founding king storyline has to end sometime for Elijah's opponents, but not this week. Sampson & Dylan-lila in a comfortable one.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) vs I still own you (John V)

The Battle of the Johns, reborn once again, and John S leads this one three games to one with a 10.96 margin from 2024 week fourteen. John S trots out Brock Purdy, Jeremiyah Love, and the Eagles' worst nightmare in A.J. Brown and Amon Ra St. Brown, a lineup that actually looks pretty sharp, maybe his best of the season. John V, meanwhile, has Player 0 listed in not one but two starting slots, which I believe is fantasy football's version of forfeiting a boxing match in your bathrobe. With Ja'Marr Chase, Breece Hall, and Quinshon Judkins all sitting on his bench behind literal empty roster spots, John V needs to find his waiver wire or his sanity before Sunday, whichever comes first. This one should belong to How bout them Cowboys, and not by a little.

Matt's moms name (Max) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

Max trails this series zero games to three, including a 51.86 point humiliation in 2023 that I assume Max has sought therapy for, and Dallas arrives at five and one looking every bit the buzzsaw. Dallas has Joe Burrow and Saquon Barkley rolling, with Tee Higgins and Sam LaPorta rounding out a lineup with no real holes, though stashing Mike Evans and Stefon Diggs on the bench behind Kendre Miller feels like Dallas simply has too many good players and not enough sympathy for the rest of us. Max counters with Bijan Robinson and Alvin Kamara, a strong backfield, but benching Jayden Daniels for C.J. Stroud is a defensible if unspectacular call. The history says blowout, the talent gap says it could happen again. JS N My Pants in a laugher.

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) vs Will the Thrill (Tim)

Two teams at two and four, both desperate, with Matt R leading the series two games to one and a 53.96 point massacre back in 2023 looming large. Matt R starts Michael Penix Jr. and leans on Bucky Irving and Jaxon Smith Njigba, solid enough, though Trevor Lawrence rotting on the bench behind Penix is a quiet little subplot worth watching. Tim counters with Kyler Murray, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Travis Kelce, but I have to flag starting Michael Mayer at a second tight end spot while Josh Allen and Marvin Harrison Jr. sit on the bench entirely unused, that's a lineup built by someone who forgot which sport he was managing. This one's a coin flip on paper, but I'll lean High Risk, High Reward on the strength of better bench discipline alone.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: JS N My Pants over Matt's moms name. Dallas is five and one and riding a three game series sweep for a reason.
  • Upset special: How bout them Cowboys over I still own you. John V's phantom roster spots make this less of an upset and more of a mercy killing.
  • Closest game to call: High Risk, High Reward versus Will the Thrill. Two desperate two and four teams, and either lineup could implode on its own.

The Back Page

Bold prediction: Straight up Yorkin' it rattles off a win this week behind Puka Nacua going nuclear, and I personally ascend from the two and four basement while somewhere Elijah, Dallas, and Austin keep trading ledger entries at the top like the rest of us are just here to keep the lights on. The dynasty watch isn't over, it's just napping.
