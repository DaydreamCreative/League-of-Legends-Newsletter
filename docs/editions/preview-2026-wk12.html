Week Twelve: Six Games, One Villain, and a Thursday Night Reckoning

Gather round, degenerates. Week twelve is upon us and the league looks like a traffic jam at a tollbooth, half of you sitting at five and five like it's some kind of fantasy purgatory, while up top JS N My Pants rides a seven and three record like Dallas Dickinson personally invented the forward pass. Meanwhile down in the basement, yours truly sits at four and six, which I will remind you is simply a slump for a three time champion and not, as my critics suggest, a pattern. I have a lineup this week that I genuinely believe can turn the corner, and I am contractually and spiritually obligated to tell you so. Let's get into every matchup before Thursday Night Football makes liars or prophets of us all.

The Matchups

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs Matt's moms name (Max)

I lead this series three to one, including a 49.14 point beatdown back in 2024, and I intend to remind Max of that history personally. My lineup has Tyler Shough at quarterback, which is a bit of a leap of faith, and I'll admit Matthew Stafford sitting on my bench is the kind of decision that keeps a man up at night. But I've got Malik Nabers, Ryan Flournoy and a three man running back committee of Jonah Coleman, Seth McGowan and Travis Etienne Jr. that should move the chains even if Shough has his moments. Max counters with Jayden Daniels slinging it to Nico Collins and Denzel Boston, plus Bijan Robinson and Alvin Kamara in the backfield, which is a genuinely stacked lineup with no real holes I can poke at, unfortunately. This is close. I'm picking myself, because of course I am, but I'll admit it's closer than my ego wants it to be.

F*q it we hibernate 🧸 (Ryan) vs Lamb to the Slaughter (Elijah)

Ryan leads this series three games to two with a 50.76 point mauling in the bank from 2024, and he brings Patrick Mahomes and Christian McCaffrey into this one, which is the kind of one two punch that wins weeks by itself. Elijah counters with Jared Goff, Jonathan Taylor, Ashton Jeanty and CeeDee Lamb, a lineup that looks like a cheat code on paper. I do want to flag that Ryan is sitting Puka Nacua on his bench behind a receiver corps of Rome Odunze and Luther Burden III, which feels like it could age poorly given Puka's ceiling, but I understand wanting to ride the hot hand. Elijah has the better record at six and four and the deeper roster, and I think his two headed backfield monster of Taylor and Jeanty carries the day here.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

Dallas sits atop the entire league at seven and three with over a thousand points on the year, and even though Erica technically trails this head to head series two games to three, she's riding a 6 and 4 record of her own and has Justin Herbert, Rashee Rice and Zay Flowers humming right now. Dallas counters with Joe Burrow, Saquon Barkley and a two tight end luxury of Isaiah Likely and Sam LaPorta. I'll note Dallas is sitting Cooper Kupp on the bench, which is fine given how deep this lineup already runs, that's a "good problems to have" situation, not a lineup mistake. This is the marquee game of the week and I think the league leader proves why he's the league leader. Give me Dallas in a tight one.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs I still own you (John V)

Austin leads this series three to one with a 49.32 point blowout in his pocket from 2024, and both teams enter at six and four, basically mirror images of each other in the standings. Austin leans on Derrick Henry and Omarion Hampton at running back plus George Pickens and Jaylen Waddle, a lineup with legitimate thunder. John V counters with Ja'Marr Chase, Brock Bowers and Breece Hall, and I have to say, Aaron Rodgers at quarterback over Jordan Love sitting on his bench is a choice I find bold given Love's recent form, but Rodgers still has the arm talent to make it work against a shaky Hampton Water secondary matchup. This one feels like a coin flip, but I'll lean toward the history and take Austin to extend his series stranglehold.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) vs High Risk, High Reward (Matt R)

John S is languishing at two and eight and still somehow leads this head to head series three games to two, which tells you how long and strange this rivalry has been. He starts Dak Prescott, Amon-Ra St. Brown and Ladd McConkey, a solid receiving corps, but Josh Jacobs rotting on his bench behind Tyler Allgeier and Rachaad White at running back is a head scratcher I can't let slide, Jacobs has simply been the better back this season. Matt R, despite a pedestrian five and five record, is quietly fourth in total points in the entire league, over one thousand on the year, and starts Trevor Lawrence, Bucky Irving and Jaxon Smith-Njigba. The talent gap here favors Matt R regardless of what the record says. I'm taking High Risk, High Reward to keep John S searching for answers.

It might be #over (Drew) vs Will the Thrill (Tim)

Drew trails this series badly, one win to five, and sits at three and seven on the year, while Tim is a far healthier five and five. Drew starts Bo Nix, Justin Jefferson and Cam Skattebo, but I have to flag Lamar Jackson sitting on his bench behind Bo Nix at quarterback, that is a swap I would be making before Sunday if I were Drew, Lamar's weekly ceiling dwarfs what Nix offers. Tim meanwhile rolls out Josh Allen, Jahmyr Gibbs and Travis Kelce, a lineup that doesn't have a glaring weakness anywhere. History and roster strength both point the same direction here. Give me Tim comfortably.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Will the Thrill over It might be #over. Tim's lineup is clean top to bottom and the five game series lead doesn't hurt either.
  • Upset special: Serving Punt over JS N My Pants. Dallas is the league's best team on paper, but Erica's been quietly building a six and four record and Herbert torching a tired secondary on a short week feels plausible.
  • Closest game to call: Straight up Yorkin' it versus Matt's moms name. I'm picking my own team, obviously, but Max's lineup genuinely has no weak spot and I'll be sweating this one in real time.

The Back Page

Bold prediction: Straight up Yorkin' it climbs off the mat this week, Shough quiets every doubter including the one typing this sentence, and I personally hand deliver a 49.14 point margin flashback to Max by Monday morning. Write it down. Frame it. I will be bringing it up in every newsletter until the league forgets I'm at four and six.
