By Matthew Waters

Week Six: The Villain Gets Thrown Off His Own Mountain

Let me get the ugly part out of the way first, because I promised you honesty even when it costs me. Straight up Yorkin' it, my proud flagship, my pride and joy, got absolutely toasted by 41.16 points this week. I will have more thoughts on that in a moment, once I've regained the strength to type about it. But the bigger story of Week Six is that the league got shaken up like a snow globe in a paint mixer: three teams sitting at five and one, a 2 and 4 squad somehow still over 596 points for the year, and a basement team that just leapt four spots in the standings without even winning the championship of anything. Buckle in. It's a mess down here, and for once, I'm standing in the middle of it.

The Recaps

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) 114.34, Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) 73.18

I'll own this one because the by laws of this newsletter demand it. Trevor Lawrence threw for 27.34 for Matt R, Jaxon Smith Njigba put up 19.4, and Carnell Tate chipped in 16.0, and when your whole lineup is clicking like that, 114 points is just what happens. Meanwhile my side of the ledger featured Juwan Johnson contributing a flat zero at tight end, which is not what I drafted a tight end to do, and Davante Adams limping to 5.4. The real crime, though, is sitting on my own bench: Tyler Shough put up 26.96 points as my backup quarterback while Matthew Stafford gave me 15.88 starting. That is an eleven point unforced error of my own doing, and I have nobody to blame but the man typing this sentence. The series is tied at two apiece now, which stings almost as much as the final score.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) 128.8, Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) 81.76

Ryan put on an absolute clinic here, the kind of week that makes you wonder if he's been saving it up. Christian McCaffrey exploded for 25.2, D'Andre Swift chipped in 21.0 out of the flex, Malik Nabers went for 22.5, and Kyle Pitts turned in a tight end line of 20.5 that most WR1s would be proud of. That's four players over twenty points on one roster. Erica got a fine floor game from James Cook at 22.8 and J.K. Dobbins at 12.1, but Deshaun Watson's 9.46 at quarterback and a near-invisible Harold Fannin at 1.4 buried her before she even had a chance to climb out. The widest margin in this head to head's history, a cool 47.04, just got set this very week.

It might be #over (Drew) 90.82, Hampton Water (Austin) 80.3

Kenneth Walker went off for 26.9 and carried Drew's whole operation, with Dameon Pierce quietly adding 13.0 even as Cam Skattebo delivered an invisible zero. Austin got a strong 23.8 from Drake Maye and 15.4 from George Kittle, but the backbreaker was sitting Tre Tucker, who dropped 24.2 on Austin's bench while Woody Marks only returned 9.1 in the lineup. That is better than fifteen points left to rot, and in a game decided by ten and a half, that's the whole ballgame. Drew moves to 2 and 4 but can at least say he out-managed somebody this week.

Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) 72.82, How bout them Cowboys (John S) 58.26

The fallen founding king keeps climbing. Jonathan Taylor went for 19.1 and Ashton Jeanty added 13.8 for Elijah, even while his quarterback Jacoby Brissett mustered a measly 6.92 and Darren Waller contributed nothing at all. John S, meanwhile, got buried by his own bench: Alvin Kamara sat there with 13.9 points unused while Rachaad White only gave him 13.7 in the actual lineup, basically a wash, but Dak Prescott's 11.02 rotting on the pine while Brock Purdy only matched it with 17.36 made the gap worse than it needed to be. A.J. Brown's 3.2 and Ladd McConkey's 2.2 were the real season killers here. Elijah's now 5 and 1 and sitting atop the standings, proof the redemption arc is very much alive.

I still own you (John V) 87.24, Matt's moms name (Max) 84.5

This one came down to the wire. Jordan Love threw for 20.24 and Matthew Golden hauled in 19.4 for John V, who got just enough to survive. Max actually out gunned him in spots, with Bhayshul Tuten erupting for 22.7, but then gave a chunk of it back by sitting C.J. Stroud's 22.98 on the bench behind Jayden Daniels's 21.0, a near coin flip that didn't need to be made, and watching DeVonta Smith's 17.0 do nothing for him from the pine while Courtland Sutton mustered only 2.9 in the actual lineup. John V also left real points on his own bench, with Germie Bernard dropping 20.2 unused, but it didn't matter this time. He moves up three spots in the standings off this win, which tells you how tight the middle of this league has become.

JS N My Pants (Dallas) 95.42, Will the Thrill (Tim) 54.1

This was a bloodbath, and the widest margin in this rivalry's history, 41.32 points, was set this very week. Isaiah Likely went bananas for 20.9 at tight end, Chuba Hubbard added 18.6, and Kirk Cousins chipped in a tidy 18.52 for Dallas. Tim's side of the ledger was a graveyard: Najee Harris produced 0.2 points, Brandin Cooks gave him 1.8, and Kalif Raymond added only 2.8. Josh Allen's 18.3 was the lone bright spot on a roster that otherwise looked like it got hit by a truck. Tim falls to 2 and 4 and trails this series 1 to 5 now, a number that should probably come with a warning label.

Standings and Movers

Sitting at the top: Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) at 5 and 1, up a spot after knocking off John S. Right behind at 5 and 1 is JS N My Pants (Dallas), also climbing a spot after that demolition of Will the Thrill. Hampton Water (Austin) holds third at 5 and 1 despite the loss to Drew, though that bench mismanagement cost him two spots in the standings, the week's single biggest fall alongside the two teams below me. Matt's moms name (Max) and Serving Punt (Erica) sit unchanged at 3 and 3 apiece.

The real headline is the bottom half of the standings turning into a game of musical chairs. I Still Own You (John V) jumped three full spots on the back of that nail biter over Max. Hawk Tua on my Penix (Ryan) rocketed up four spots after that 128.8 explosion, the single biggest riser of the week, and High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) climbed two spots by handing me my own eyeballs on a platter. On the way down, Will the Thrill (Tim) and How bout them Cowboys (John S) both fell three spots after ugly losses, and my own Straight up Yorkin' it crew dropped three spots as well, which I will be taking up with my fantasy therapist this week.

Looking Ahead

Week Seven has a genuine heavyweight bout at the top, with JS N My Pants and Sampson & Dylan-lila, both 5 and 1, finally colliding to settle who really runs this league right now. Elsewhere, I get Hampton Water and his 5 and 1 record, which should be a lovely chance to prove last week was a fluke and not a pattern. The Battle of the Johns adjacent rivalries keep simmering too, with How bout them Cowboys hosting Matt's moms name, and Drew trying to climb out of the basement against I still own you.

The Back Page

Look, I know how this looks. The winningest manager in league history, three rings on the mantle, 2019 and 2021 and 2023, sitting here at 2 and 4 with Tyler Shough mocking me from my own bench. I'm not going to pretend that's not embarrassing. But a dynasty isn't built in Week Six, it's built in the fourth quarter of the season when everybody else has forgotten how to play defense against me. Mark these words down in the record book right next to my 1753.72 points from 2019: the villain is just getting warmed up. Everybody else should enjoy first place while it lasts.
