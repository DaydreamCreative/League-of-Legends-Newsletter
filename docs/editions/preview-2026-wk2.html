Week Two: Redemption Songs, Battle of the Johns, and My Own Team Needs an Intervention

Here is the thing about a 0 and 1 start when you are the winningest manager in the history of this operation: everybody loves it. My group chat has been positively gleeful. "The GOAT is human," they say, like I have not already got three rings sitting in a drawer collecting dust while some of these men are still hunting their first. I will address my own house of horrors shortly, because it needs addressing, but the bigger story of Week Two is that half this league is undefeated and feeling frisky, and the other half is one bad Thursday from a genuine crisis. Let's get into it.

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs I still own you (John V)

I am contractually obligated by my own ego to cover this fairly, so let me just say it: my Week One was a disaster and I am not hiding from it. Sixty nine and change points is not a number, it is a cry for help. John V is in the same leaky boat at 0 and 1, and I own this series 3 to 1 historically, including a 49.2 point beatdown back in 2024, so there is precedent for me to feel good here. But I have questions about my own lineup. Cam Skattebo is out there starting over Jonathon Brooks and Josh Jacobs sitting on my bench, and I need someone to explain to me why Quentin Johnston is in my starting lineup over Ja'Kobi Lane or even Wan'Dale Robinson in a what should be a pass funnel week. Meanwhile John V is starting Jacory Croskey-Merritt over a healthy Rico Dowdle, which feels like a coin flip he did not need to make, and he is sitting Brock Bowers, one of the best tight ends alive, behind Dallas Goedert. That is the kind of decision that gets a manager a strongly worded text from his own family. Two shaky lineups, one desperate columnist. I will take myself here, mostly because I refuse to print my own doom in back to back weeks. Pick: Straight up Yorkin' it.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) vs Matt's moms name (Max)

Ryan enters 0 and 1 and trailing this series 2 to 3, and honestly his lineup looks fine, Mahomes slinging it to Smith-Njigba and DeVonta Smith is a solid floor, though I will note he is sitting A.J. Brown for Devaughn Vele and Luther Burden, which is the kind of boom or bust gamble that either wins you the column inches or gets you a strongly worded angry text from Philadelphia's finest. Max is 1 and 0 off a strong debut and has arguably the cleanest lineup in the league this week, Jayden Daniels, Bijan, Nico Collins, nothing to squint at. The widest margin in this series was 40.7 points and either side is capable of that kind of number on a good day. Pick: Matt's moms name, because clean lineups beat interesting ones more often than not.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs Real Eaters (Drew)

This series is knotted at two apiece, which tells you nothing and everything at once. Erica starts 0 and 1 and I like her receiving corps, Rashee Rice and Drake London are legitimate problems for any defense, but she is sitting Harold Fannin behind Dalton Kincaid at tight end, and I am not sure that is the right call given the target competition Kincaid is dealing with. Drew is 1 and 0 and riding Lamar Jackson and Justin Jefferson, which is about as strong a QB1 WR1 combo as exists in this league, though stashing Brian Thomas on the bench behind Rome Odunze and Jalen Coker is a decision I would be nervous about if I were him. Widest margin in this rivalry is a gaudy 48.22, so blowouts are not unheard of here. Pick: Real Eaters, Lamar just does too much.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs How bout them Cowboys (John S)

Two undefeated teams collide, and John S is sitting atop the entire standings after a 154.36 opening explosion, so this is the marquee matchup of the week whether the league wants to admit it or not. Austin trails the all time series 2 to 3 but is riding a nice one week high with Derrick Henry and Omarion Hampton giving him a two headed ground attack that should scare anybody. His lineup looks solid, though I do wonder if RJ Harvey deserves more volume than a complementary role suggests. John S, meanwhile, has Amon-Ra St. Brown and Ladd McConkey both going, and Caleb Williams under center, and I don't see an obvious mistake in that lineup, which is more than I can say for some other managers I could mention, myself very much included. History says this series can get gruesome, 35.72 points wide back in 2023. Pick: How bout them Cowboys, on the strength of that Week One number alone.

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

Matt R is staring down one of the uglier historical marks in this whole preview, 0 and 4 all time against Dallas, with a 45.22 point beatdown on the books from 2023. Rough. His lineup this week has Christian McCaffrey and Ashton Jeanty both going, which is a nice foundation, but he is sitting Trevor Lawrence, fine, and rolling with Jared Goff, also fine, so no real complaints there beyond the fact that history itself seems allergic to him beating Dallas. Dallas is 1 and 0 with Joe Burrow throwing to Tee Higgins and Tetairoa McMillan, and stashing Alvin Kamara and Stefon Diggs on the bench feels like a luxury problem, not a mistake. Pick: JS N My Pants, the streak lives.

Will the Thrill (Tim) vs Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah)

Elijah is the fallen founding king still hunting his way back to 2016 glory, and a 1 and 0 start with Jalen Hurts and Jonathan Taylor humming along is a nice start to that redemption arc. Tim trails this head to head 1 to 3 with a genuinely brutal 56.18 point margin somewhere in the family history, and I have real concerns about his lineup this week. Josh Allen and Jahmyr Gibbs are locks, no argument, but starting Alec Pierce over Xavier Worthy or Marvin Harrison feels like a mistake dressed up as a plan, and Jadarian Price ahead of a healthy Tony Pollard on the bench is the kind of thing that makes a beat reporter raise an eyebrow. Pick: Sampson & Dylan-lila, and I think it is not particularly close.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: How bout them Cowboys over Hampton Water. John S looked like the best team in the league in Week One and nothing in Austin's lineup scares me enough to bet against it.
  • Upset special: Hawk Tua on my Penix over Matt's moms name. Max's lineup is cleaner on paper, but Ryan has the higher ceiling if Mahomes and Smith-Njigba get rolling, and undefeated teams have a way of getting caught looking ahead.
  • Closest game to call: Serving Punt vs Real Eaters. Dead even history, dead even vibes, and I would not bet real money on this one either way.

The Back Page

Bold prediction, and yes I am aware how this looks coming from the guy writing the column: Straight up Yorkin' it rights the ship this week, Puka Nacua and Davante Adams both go off, and I quietly climb out of the basement while somewhere Elijah Cohen's redemption tour and John S's runaway train both keep rolling right along without me. Three championships do not just disappear because of one bad Thursday reading, and this league is about to remember exactly why they were so happy to see me stumble in the first place.
