Week Two: Everybody's Undefeated Except the People Who Aren't

Here is the beautiful, cruel arithmetic of Week Two. Six teams sit at one and oh, feeling like geniuses. Six teams sit at zero and one, already Googling "is it too early to panic in fantasy football." I happen to manage one of the latter. I am not proud of this. But I am the guy writing the newsletter, and the pen, as they say, is mightier than the sword and also mightier than my 69.6 points from Week One, which I would very much like everyone to forget. Let's get into a slate that features the Battle of the Johns brewing on the horizon, a winless Matt R still hunting his first ever win against Dallas Dickinson in a series that has been an unbroken chain of misery, and yours truly attempting to climb out of the cellar against a man I have beaten three times out of four all time. Onward.

The Matchups

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs I still own you (John V)

Let's start with the marquee matchup, and I say that with zero bias whatsoever. I lead this series three to one, including a 49.2 point beatdown back in 2024, and I fully intend to remind John V of that fact early and often. Both of us stumbled out of the gate at zero and one, which means somebody's going to feel a lot better by Friday morning and somebody's going to feel a lot worse. John V, of course, is the man with a championship ring from 2020 and yet the worst all time winning percentage among active managers, a stat I bring up not to be cruel but because it is simply true and I am contractually obligated as your local beat reporter to be truthful. History and, frankly, destiny favor Straight up Yorkin' it. My pick: Matt W.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) vs Matt's moms name (Max)

Ryan enters this one trailing the all time series two to three, still searching for his signature win of the year despite being the proud owner of a "best team to never win a title" reputation that follows him around like a rain cloud. Max, meanwhile, is undefeated and sitting pretty in fourth place after a strong Week One. The widest margin in this series was a gaudy 40.7 points just last year, so these two clearly know how to put on a show. Ryan's got the swagger of a former top scorer, but Max has the early season mojo. I'll take Max to move to two and oh.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs Real Eaters (Drew)

This series is dead even at two and two, and it has produced some absolute violence, including a 48.22 point blowout back in 2023. Erica, the 2019 runner up, is looking to shake off an oh and one start, while Drew rides in fresh off a strong Week One and sitting second in the standings. Drew also happens to be the reigning 2022 Sleeper era champion, a man with the lowest career win percentage among the vets who somehow still found a way to hoist a trophy, proof that this league has no respect for résumés. I like Drew to keep rolling here.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs How bout them Cowboys (John S)

Two undefeated teams collide, and something has got to give. John S sits atop the standings with a gaudy 154.36 points already banked, the best mark in the league through one week. Austin trails this head to head series two to three and has been hunting a championship since finishing runner up all the way back in 2016, a decade long quest that has produced a lot of near misses and, apparently, a very nice bottle of rosé named after his team. The widest margin here was 35.72 points back in 2023, so blowouts are very much on the table. I have to side with the hot hand. Give me John S.

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

I almost feel bad previewing this one. Matt R is oh and four all time against Dallas, with the widest margin in that ugly history sitting at 45.22 points. Matt R also dropped his Week One game, so he arrives here zero and one and staring down a rivalry that has given him absolutely nothing to celebrate. Dallas, on the other hand, is one and oh and probably feeling pretty comfortable given the historical trend line. I would love to tell you this is the week Matt R breaks through. History says otherwise. My pick: Dallas, again.

Will the Thrill (Tim) vs Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah)

Tim, Mr. Consistent himself and the 2021 runner up, trails this series one to three and is coming off a Week One loss despite posting a very respectable 118.36 points on the young season, more than several teams that actually won. Elijah, the fallen founding king of 2016 who has been searching for his redemption arc for the better part of a decade, is one and oh and looking to build momentum. The widest margin in this series was a jaw dropping 56.18 points back in 2023, so buckle up. I'll take Elijah to move to two and oh, but I would not be shocked if Tim's residual Week One points prove he was the better team all along.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) over I still own you (John V). I have the all time edge, the motivation, and frankly the newsletter, so take that for what it's worth.
  • Upset special: Matt R finally snaps the oh and four curse against Dallas. It has to happen eventually. Maybe not this week. But a man can dream, and so can Matt R.
  • Too close to call: Hampton Water (Austin) vs How bout them Cowboys (John S), two undefeated squads with a history of double digit swings in either direction.

The Back Page

Bold prediction time, and shockingly it involves me. After an embarrassing zero and one start that saw Straight up Yorkin' it post the lowest score in the entire league, I am calling my shot: I beat John V, climb out of the basement, and remind this league exactly why the trophy case at my house has three rings in it. Meanwhile, keep an eye on the top of the standings, because John S at 154.36 points looks like he's playing a different sport than the rest of us, and if nobody slows him down soon we might be looking at a full blown dynasty crisis that isn't even mine for once.
