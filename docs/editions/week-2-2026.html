Week Two Kicks Off With Everybody Stuck at Zero, Which Somehow Includes Me

Gather close, degenerates, because I have bad news wrapped in worse news. It is Week Two of the 2026 campaign and every single matchup on the board reads zero to zero as I file this, which either means my sources filed this newsletter suspiciously early or the entire league collectively forgot how to play football at the same time. I am going to assume the former, because the alternative is too depressing to write about, and I am contractually obligated as both reporter and reigning three time champion to keep morale up.

The real story of the week is not on the scoreboard yet, it is in the schedule itself. My own Straight up Yorkin' it enters at zero and one and staring down I still own you, a series I lead three games to one, and if my Kyren Williams and Matthew Stafford show up the way champions are supposed to show up, this one writes itself. Meanwhile at the top of the standings, three teams sit undefeated with real points on the board, John Sagherian's How bout them Cowboys lead the entire league at one hundred fifty four and change, and everybody else is left wondering how big that gap gets by Thanksgiving.

Around the League

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs I still own you (John V)

I am not going to pretend I am unbiased here, mostly because I am not, but the history does the talking for me. I lead this series three games to one, with a widest margin of forty nine point two back in 2024, which if memory serves was the week John V went home and reconsidered some life choices. Both of us sit at zero and one after Week One, so this is less a measuring contest and more a fight to avoid the league basement.

John V leans on Jordan Love, Breece Hall, and Quinshon Judkins, a trio that sounds scary on paper but has produced exactly nothing so far. I am rolling with Stafford, Kyren Williams, and Javonte Williams, and frankly I like my chances of extending the series lead to four and one. Call it homerism, call it prophecy, I call it Tuesday.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) vs Matt's moms name (Max)

Ryan Bartlett is the perennial best team to never win a pre Sleeper ring, and he opens this season zero and one, trailing Max in their head to head series two games to three. Max, fresh off a Week One win with Jayden Daniels, Bijan Robinson, and Bucky Irving humming along, is sitting pretty at one and zero and looks like he wants to make it two straight.

The widest margin in this rivalry was forty point seven back in 2025, so these two know how to put a beating on each other when it gets going. Ryan counters with Dak Prescott, Kenny Gainwell, and David Montgomery, a solid crew but one that needs to actually produce this week. I will say this, Ryan has a knack for the big score when nobody expects it, but I am leaning Max to move to two and zero.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs Real Eaters (Drew)

Erica Hallam, the 2019 runner up and steady hand of this league, finds herself at zero and one and needs a response fast against Drew Knight, who is undefeated and sitting second in the standings behind his Lamar Jackson led attack. This series is knotted at two apiece, with Erica's widest margin of victory sitting at a gaudy forty eight point two two back in 2023.

Erica trots out Justin Herbert, James Cook, and Jaylen Warren, while Drew counters with Lamar Jackson, Kenneth Walker, and Travis Etienne, a lineup that has already banked over one hundred thirty points on the season. History says this series is a coin flip, but momentum says Drew rolls to two and zero.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs How bout them Cowboys (John S)

Your unbeaten league leader John Sagherian, half of the Battle of the Johns lore and owner of one hundred fifty four points already banked, runs into Austin Hermann, the 2016 runner up still hunting his first ring a full decade later. Both sides enter one and zero, which means somebody's perfect record dies this week.

Austin leans on Drake Maye, Derrick Henry, and his namesake Omarion Hampton, while John S counters with Caleb Williams, Chase Brown, and D'Andre Swift. John S leads this all time series three games to two, and given he is the hottest team in the league right now, I do not love Austin's odds, but that widest margin of thirty five point seven two from 2023 proves Austin has the ceiling to shock somebody.

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

Here is a rivalry nobody wants to be on the wrong end of. Matt R trails this series zero games to four, an ugly stat made uglier by a forty five point two two blowout back in 2023. Matt R sits at zero and one to open the year, and now walks into the mouth of the one opponent who has simply never lost to him.

Christian McCaffrey and Ashton Jeanty give Matt R a puncher's chance, but Dallas enters one and zero off a solid Week One and has the psychological edge that comes from four straight wins in this matchup. Until Matt R actually beats this man, I have to pick Dallas, history is undefeated and I am not betting against it.

Will the Thrill (Tim) vs Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah)

Tim Gravely, Mr. Consistent himself and the 2021 runner up, opens zero and one and now faces Elijah Cohen, the fallen founding king trying to claw his way back from the depths of 2020 and 2021. Elijah sits one and zero and third in the standings, looking like a man on a redemption arc.

Tim trails this series one game to three, with a brutal widest margin of fifty six point one eight back in 2023 still on the books. Josh Allen and Jahmyr Gibbs give Tim firepower, but Elijah's Jalen Hurts and Jonathan Taylor look every bit as scary. I think the ghost of 2016 Elijah shows up this week and pushes him to two and zero.
