Week Ten: A Thursday Full of Desperate Men and One Suspiciously Confident Columnist

Here is the state of the League of Legends as the lights come up on Week Ten: six teams sit within a single game of five and four, two teams are duking it out at the top with matching six and three records, and my own Straight up Yorkin' it squad is treading water at four and five like a man who insists he's "fine" while holding his side. There is no bigger storyline this week than the sheer number of .500ish teams about to cannibalize each other, because by Monday night a good chunk of this middle class is either climbing into playoff position or sliding quietly toward the John S basement. Let's get into it, game by game, with the appropriate amount of homerism for my own matchup and the appropriate amount of honesty for everyone else's questionable lineup decisions.

The Matchups

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs Will the Thrill (Tim)

Let the record show I am a three time champion writing about my own team with zero conflict of interest whatsoever. Both clubs stumble in at four and five, and Tim actually owns me in this series, three wins to one, including a 34.28 point beatdown back in 2023 that I still think about in the shower. But here's my concern for Tim: Josh Allen and Jahmyr Gibbs are a fine floor, yet he's got Parker Washington and Adonai Mitchell starting at wide receiver while Xavier Worthy and Marvin Harrison Jr. rot on his bench. That is not a lineup, Tim, that is a cry for help. Meanwhile I'll admit my own receiver room is a bit of a horror show too, Ryan Flournoy and Konata Mumpfield starting over Tyreek Hill and Puka Nacua is the kind of decision that gets a manager's name whispered in disgust at the league dinner, and frankly I should know better. Both of us have better players sitting in street clothes. But I've got Kyren Williams and A.J. Brown anchoring things, and I trust my own gut over Tim's receiver corps of mystery men. Pick: Straight up Yorkin' it, narrowly, mostly on vibes and homerism.

F*q it we bears 🧸 (Ryan) vs It might be #over (Drew)

Ryan trails this head to head one to two but has the far scarier roster on paper, Mahomes and McCaffrey still carry weight even in a down year, though starting Darren Waller and Mack Hollins next to Kyle Pitts feels like settling for leftovers when Rome Odunze and Michael Pittman Jr. are sitting right there on his own bench. Drew, for his part, is playing with fire by starting Drew Lock over Bo Nix and Lamar Jackson, which is either a bold loyalty move or a cry for help depending on how charitable you're feeling, and at three and six Drew needs every edge he can get. Justin Jefferson keeps him afloat, but a quarterback named Drew Lock piloting the "It might be #over" offense feels thematically perfect and competitively alarming. Pick: F*q it we bears, Mahomes should be enough.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs I still own you (John V)

This series is deadlocked two and two, and John V enters red hot at six and three, riding Aaron Rodgers and Ja'Marr Chase into a matchup where Erica's Deshaun Watson led attack feels like it's bringing a knife to a gunfight. Erica does have some sneaky depth on her bench, Justin Herbert and Austin Ekeler watching from the sideline while Watson and Jaylen Warren start feels like a quiet admission that this roster is running on fumes at the skill positions that matter. John V, meanwhile, looks sharp across the board, Judkins, Breece Hall, Chase, and Bowers is a legitimately scary lineup with no obvious correction needed. Pick: I still own you, and the name suggests John V believes it too.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs Lamb to the Slaughter (Elijah)

Poor Austin. He trails this series zero and three, including a 36.1 point massacre back in 2024, and Elijah's crew comes in at six and three looking like the league's quiet juggernaut with Jonathan Taylor, Ashton Jeanty, and CeeDee Lamb all humming. Austin's lineup is at least sound, Derrick Henry and Omarion Hampton give him a real two headed backfield, and George Kittle at tight end is as safe as it gets, but this is a talent gap the box score can't paper over. Elijah has Braelon Allen and Jalen Hurts stashed on his bench and still doesn't need them, which tells you everything about the gulf here. Pick: Lamb to the Slaughter continues the sweep.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

John S is two and seven and has lost this head to head four times out of five, including a 53.5 point avalanche just last year, so the vibes here are not good. Worse, he's got Corey Kiner and Audric Estime starting at running back while Josh Jacobs sits on his bench, which is the kind of start sit decision that makes a beat reporter wince and reach for another coffee. Dallas, sitting pretty at six and three and tied for the league lead in points, countered by stacking three tight ends, LaPorta, Likely, and Hockenson, which is either genius matchup theory or a man who forgot how many wide receivers exist, especially with Mike Evans and Cooper Kupp both benched. Odd roster construction aside, the talent gap still favors Dallas comfortably. Pick: JS N My Pants.

Matt's moms name (Max) vs High Risk, High Reward (Matt R)

Max leads this series two to one but Matt R has a real chance to flip the script, he's quietly sitting on a league best 944.12 points scored despite a four and five record, which means he's been the unluckiest team in the building. His lineup looks fine too, Trevor Lawrence spreading it to Jaxon Smith Njigba and Carnell Tate while Bucky Irving curiously rides the bench behind Blake Corum and Ollie Gordon, a decision I'd keep an eye on. Max counters with Jayden Daniels and Bijan Robinson, a strong floor, and benching C.J. Stroud for Daniels is the correct and unremarkable call. This is the closest matchup on the board by scoring pedigree alone. Pick: High Risk, High Reward finally cashes in on all those phantom points.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Lamb to the Slaughter over Hampton Water, Elijah has owned this series and has the deeper roster top to bottom.
  • Upset special: How bout them Cowboys keeping it close against JS N My Pants, if Dallas's three tight end experiment goes sideways, John S could sneak a backdoor cover even in a loss.
  • Too close to call: Matt's moms name versus High Risk, High Reward, a series rivalry with a hard luck case on one side and momentum on the other.

The Back Page

Bold prediction: Straight up Yorkin' it snaps its four game funk, Tyreek Hill stays benched for no good reason and I complain about it in print next week anyway, and somewhere in the standings Matt R finally converts his league leading point total into a win that actually moves the needle. Dynasty watch continues. The crown stays mine until further notice.
