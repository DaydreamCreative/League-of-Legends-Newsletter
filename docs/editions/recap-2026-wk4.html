By Matthew Waters

Week Four: The Dynasty Takes an L, Hampton Water Stays Perfect

Let me get this out of the way with the dignity of a man who has three rings and nothing left to prove: I lost. Straight up Yorkin' it fell 114.28 to 141.52 against JS N My Pants, and yes, I will still be covering this week with complete objectivity, because that is the kind of professional I am. Meanwhile, up at the top of the standings, Hampton Water is sitting there at four and zero like they invented the sport, and the rest of the league is starting to resemble a demolition derby where everybody's record is tangled up at two and two or worse. Let's get into it, and I promise to only cry a little.

The Recaps

JS N My Pants 141.52, Straight up Yorkin' it 114.28

I want to be mad at Dallas, but Tetairoa McMillan hung 37.2 on me and there is simply no legislating against that kind of violence. Chuba Hubbard added 25.4 and Joe Burrow quietly went for 22.72, and that trio buried me before I even got out of bed. On my side, Kyren Williams was a bright spot at 31.7 and Javonte Williams chipped in 28.8, and Puka Nacua was steady at 23.2, so it is not like my studs no showed. The dagger was Davante Adams putting up a punchless 5.2 and Chris Bell going for a flat zero, and I'll admit leaving Tyler Shough on my bench while Matthew Stafford limped to 9.68 is the kind of quarterback decision that keeps a man up at night. Dallas also left Sam Darnold's 13.32 and T.J. Hockenson's 18.4 in street clothes, so even the winner left some meat on the bone. History now sits at Dallas leading the series three to one, and I don't want to talk about it further.

How bout them Cowboys 85.42, Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ 71.7

Ryan's night was sunk by a bench that was actively laughing at him, Brian Robinson posted 25.2 and Emanuel Wilson added 24.5 and neither one saw the field while David Montgomery mustered only 4.3 and Bucky Irving limped in at 6.1. That is nearly 50 points of cold storage while his started running backs combined for barely 10. Patrick Mahomes did his part with 17.0 but it wasn't enough cover. John S won this one without needing fireworks, Brock Purdy went for 19.62 and Alvin Kamara provided the thump with 20.3, even though Ladd McConkey no showed with a zero and Parker Washington added only 1.5. Good enough to improve to two and two and keep Ryan searching for answers at one and three.

Hampton Water 103.66, Serving Punt ✨ 95.86

Austin keeps finding ways to stay undefeated, and this time it came without even needing his best arm, Drake Maye sat the bench with 23.16 passing yards worth of regret while Bryce Young started and still delivered 21.46. George Kittle chipped in 15.0 and the committee approach at running back, Derrick Henry, Omarion Hampton, and RJ Harvey, combined for over 40 points by itself. Erica fought hard, Zay Flowers was excellent at 21.8 and James Cook and Jaylen Warren both cleared 13, but Justin Herbert gave her almost nothing at 8.56 and Rashee Rice was an outright zero. Four and oh for Hampton Water now, and the league's gotta start wondering if anybody can touch them.

Matt's moms name 133.58, Sampson & Dylan-lila 114.82

Max put together his best week of the season, with Bijan Robinson going for 27.2, Nico Collins adding 27.3, and Kyle Monangai somehow matching that with 27.5 of his own, a three headed monster that buried Elijah despite a monstrous 32.8 night from CeeDee Lamb. Jonathan Taylor also went for 22.2 for Sampson & Dylan-lila, but Jalen Hurts only mustered 12.52 and Garrett Wilson was a near non factor at 4.2, and that was too much to overcome. Elijah left Romeo Doubs' 20.8 on his bench, which stings extra given the final margin. Max now leads that head to head series four to two and climbs six spots in the standings, the single biggest riser in the whole league this week.

High Risk, High Reward 104.28, Real Eaters 96.68

Kenneth Walker was an absolute workhorse for Drew with 30.4 points, but it wasn't enough support, Chris Godwin managed only 6.1 and Dalton Schultz barely registered at 1.9. Matt R got balanced production across the board, Ashton Jeanty at 15.6, Rhamondre Stevenson at 16.9, and Malik Nabers at 20.2, enough to cover for Emeka Egbuka's quiet 2.3 day. Matt R also left Jared Goff's 20.48 on his bench behind Trevor Lawrence's 13.08, a decision that worked out fine this time but won't always. Drew falls to one and three while extending his losing skid in this series to zero and five all time, which is just brutal.

I still own you 86.78, Will the Thrill 72.02

Tim got a nice floor game from Josh Allen at 17.52 and Jahmyr Gibbs at 15.7, but DJ Moore and Kalif Raymond combined for a woeful 3.8 points between two starting wide receiver spots, and that kind of production gap doesn't get overcome easily. John V rode Quinshon Judkins at 18.6 and Brock Bowers at 17.6 to the win, even though Ja'Marr Chase face planted with only 4.2. The bigger story might be what John V left behind, Romeo Doubs' 20.8 sitting unused on his bench too, so both managers in this one had points to spare in the tank. John V climbs to two and two while Tim drops to one and three.

Standings and Movers

Hampton Water sits alone at the top at four and zero, the only unbeaten left standing, with Sampson & Dylan-lila second at three and one despite the week four loss, and JS N My Pants right behind at three and one as well after knocking me off. After that it is a pileup of two and two teams, Matt's moms name, How bout them Cowboys, my own Straight up Yorkin' it, Serving Punt, and I still own you, all jammed into the middle of the table.

The biggest riser by a mile is Matt's moms name, up six spots on the strength of that 133.58 outburst, proof that Max's running back room is finally clicking. JS N My Pants and How bout them Cowboys both jumped two spots with their wins. On the way down, I have to admit my own team fell three spots after that loss to Dallas, tied with Will the Thrill and Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ for the week's biggest fallers, each dropping three spots. Serving Punt also slid three spots despite the loss being competitive. Painful company to keep, but I'll take my medicine.

Looking Ahead

Week five brings me a date with Serving Punt in a battle of two and two desperation, while Hampton Water gets a dangerous test against a hungry High Risk, High Reward squad looking to climb out of the basement. Elsewhere, the Battle adjacent subplot of John V hosting Sampson & Dylan-lila could decide who stays in the top tier, and Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ gets a brutal test against red hot JS N My Pants.

The Back Page

Look, I lost this week, I said it, I owned it, and I'll say it again so nobody accuses me of burying the lede, Straight up Yorkin' it went down 114.28 to 141.52. But let the record show I still have three championships and the best winning percentage in this league's history, and one bad Tuesday against Tetairoa McMillan's career night doesn't change the fact that I am still the villain everybody in this league is trying and failing to dethrone. Rings talk, bench production from Chris Bell walks. See everybody next week, when I fully expect to have my revenge arc back on track.
