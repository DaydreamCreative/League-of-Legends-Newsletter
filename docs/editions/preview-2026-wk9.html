Week 9 Preview, 2026November 06, 2026
By Matthew Waters

Week Nine Eve: The Dynasty Clings To Dot Five While The Rest Of You Fight Over Scraps

Gather close, degenerates, because Thursday is bearing down on us and the League of Legends sits at that beautiful midpoint of the season where hope and delusion wear the same jersey. Elijah and Dallas are tangled at the top like two heavyweights who respect each other too much to actually fight yet. Meanwhile my own Straight up Yorkin' it squad sits at four and four, which I'll remind you is simply the GOAT taking a brief sabbatical before the inevitable second half coronation. Three rings don't grow mold, people. They age like wine, or possibly like the Javonte Williams tape I've been watching, which is to say cautiously optimistic. Let's get into the slate.

The Matchups

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan)

This series is dead even at two apiece, which feels wrong given I'm the three time champion and Ryan is still searching for his first ring despite historically big scoring years. I like my roster here, and not just because I pencil it in every week with a conflict of interest the size of a Buick. Stafford slinging it to Puka Nacua and A.J. Brown is about as good as a two man show gets, and Kyren Williams gives me a stable floor Ryan has to actually respect. Now, credit where due, Ryan's lineup is loaded with name value: Christian McCaffrey, Malik Nabers, Caleb Williams. But I have to ask, out loud, in print, why Rome Odunze is glued to his bench while Michael Wilson and Luther Burden the Third start. Odunze has been the better weekly bet in that offense more often than not, and sitting him feels like a man trying to lose on purpose. I'm taking Yorkin's crew here, and not because I pay myself to write this column. Okay, partly because of that.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs Matt's moms name (Max)

Erica owns this series outright, three to nothing, with a widest margin of twenty five point six eight just last season, so Max walks in here as the historical punching bag looking to finally land one. Erica is rolling out Malik Willis at quarterback, which is always a stomach churning adventure, but she's got Rashee Rice, Drake London, and Zay Flowers to cover for his sins. Max counters with Jayden Daniels and Bijan Robinson, which on paper should be the headline act of this whole slate, yet Max is starting Bhayshul Tuten over Alvin Kamara, and I'm squinting at that one. Kamara has been the steadier weekly producer and Tuten's role still looks like a rookie timeshare. Also worth noting DeVonta Smith rotting on that bench while Courtland Sutton and Jordan Addison start feels like it could bite him. History says Erica, the eye test says Erica, I'm taking Erica.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs Will the Thrill (Tim)

Buckle up for this one because the history is unhinged. Austin trails this series one to two, but the two sides have combined for a sixty point six margin in a single game just last year, meaning somebody is capable of absolutely detonating. Austin has Derrick Henry and Omarion Hampton both in the backfield, which is a load management nightmare for Tim's defense to solve, plus Drake Maye quietly having himself a season. Tim counters with Josh Allen, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Travis Kelce, a trio that can win him the week by itself. My one raised eyebrow is Tim starting Adonai Mitchell and Makai Lemon over a bench holding Xavier Worthy and Marvin Harrison Jr., two names with far higher ceilings on a given Sunday. That's a lineup that looks built on hope rather than target share. I'll lean Hampton Water in a shootout, mostly on the strength of that double headed backfield.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) vs It might be #over (Drew)

Two and six against two and six, the kind of game nobody circles on the calendar except the two men actually playing it, and the man who has to write about it. John S trails this head to head one to three with a brutal fifty nine point three eight margin on record, so there's real scar tissue here. John S has Josh Jacobs, Amon Ra St. Brown and Brock Purdy, a lineup that should be competitive on any given week, though benching Dak Prescott for Purdy is a choice I trust given Purdy's current form. Drew meanwhile is starting Bo Nix and leaning on Justin Jefferson to be the whole offense, with Lamar Jackson curiously parked on the bench behind him, which is the kind of decision that ages like milk left on a radiator. Jefferson can take over a game by himself, but I can't back a team sitting a talent like Lamar. I'll take How bout them Cowboys to snap their skid.

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) vs Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah)

Elijah sits alone at the top of the standings at six and two, and the founding king's redemption tour from his 2016 glory days to his dark 2020 and 2021 seasons continues to pick up steam. This series is tied two and two, but Elijah brings Jonathan Taylor, CeeDee Lamb, and Ashton Jeanty into this one, which is about as complete a starting lineup as this league has to offer. Matt R counters with Bucky Irving and Jaxon Smith Njigba, both quality, but I have real questions about Rhamondre Stevenson and Blake Corum both starting in the same backfield group while Emeka Egbuka sits on the bench. Egbuka has flashed more weekly upside than either of those two backs and deserves a longer look. Matt R is a scrappy .571 all time winner, but Elijah looks like the best team in the league right now, and I'm not fighting that.

JS N My Pants (Dallas) vs I still own you (John V)

Dallas owns this series three to nothing, including a jaw dropping seventy six point two four margin blowout just last season, so John V walks into this one as the history's designated victim. But John V is five and three and playing good ball with Ja'Marr Chase, Jordan Love, and Breece Hall humming. My concern for John V is starting both Brock Bowers and Dallas Goedert at the same time, a two tight end look that caps his receiver flexibility when DK Metcalf sits idle on the bench. That's a lot of eggs in the tight end basket. Dallas counters with Joe Burrow, Saquon Barkley, and Tee Higgins, and that roster simply has no holes that I can find, which is annoying from a drama standpoint but good for Dallas. I'll ride the history and take Dallas, though John V has the pieces to finally snap the curse if the Bowers and Goedert double dip actually pays off.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: JS N My Pants over I still own you. Dallas has won this matchup three straight times by increasingly embarrassing margins and the roster depth backs it up again.
  • Upset special: How bout them Cowboys over It might be #over. Two bottom dwellers, but Drew benching Lamar Jackson for Bo Nix is the kind of self inflicted wound that decides these things.
  • Closest to call: Hampton Water versus Will the Thrill. A series that has already produced a sixty point blowout has no business being predictable, and I expect fireworks down to the last stat correction.

The Back Page

Bold prediction: Straight up Yorkin' it climbs back to five hundred this week and this is the exact game historians will point to when they write the chapter on ring number four. Write it down, frame it, I'll wait.
