Week Nine: The Beat Reporter's Impartial, Totally Unbiased Preview

Gather round, degenerates. Week nine is upon us, Thursday Night kicks it off, and I come to you this week wearing two hats: one as your humble chronicler of league history, and one as the owner of Straight up Yorkin' it, a 3 and 4 team that desperately needs to remember how to be good at this game. I will try to keep those hats separate. I will probably fail. But let's get into it, because there's a top five showdown nobody saw coming between a resurgent Hampton Water and a reeling Will the Thrill, there's a Battle of Dallas's Buzzsaw happening against a John V team that has never beaten him, and there's a game at the bottom of the standings between two 2 and 5 clubs that smells suspiciously like destiny for whoever loses it. Let's break it all down.

The Matchups

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan)

Now, I want to be very clear that I am a neutral journalist, but I also happen to be 3 and 4 and in desperate need of this one, and I happen to think my lineup is quite good this week, thank you for asking. Puka Nacua, A.J. Brown, and Ja'Kobi Lane is about as nasty a receiver room as anyone's fielding in this league, and I've got Kyren Williams and Javonte Williams in the backfield doing work. The series sits even at two apiece, with my 2023 week four demolition still the high water mark. Ryan's Hawk Tua squad is 2 and 5 and, frankly, I have some concerns about his lineup. Starting Caleb Williams over a bench that includes Patrick Mahomes is a choice. Starting D'Andre Swift and Jonah Coleman over Christian McCaffrey sitting right there is a bigger choice. I understand bye weeks and matchups complicate things, but when you've got CMC on your bench in a game you need to win, that's the kind of thing that keeps a man up at night. My pick, and I promise this is analysis and not wish casting: Straight up Yorkin' it.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs Matt's moms name (Max)

Two 4 and 3 clubs jockeying for positioning, and Erica has historically owned this matchup, up 3 to 0 all time with a 25.68 point beatdown just last season in week nine, spooky scheduling symmetry there. But I have to flag something: Erica is starting Malik Willis at quarterback while Justin Herbert sits on her bench. I don't care how good the matchup is, that is a gift to Max that he should not take lightly. On the other side, Max is rolling with Jayden Daniels, Bijan Robinson, and Trey McBride, which is a perfectly sturdy trio, though leaving Alvin Kamara and Nico Collins on his bench while starting Kyle Monangai and Khalil Shakir makes me squint a little. This is a coin flip game with one side potentially sabotaging itself at quarterback. I'll lean Matt's moms name to finally snap the skid, mostly because Malik Willis scares me more than it should.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs Will the Thrill (Tim)

Austin's been the league's quiet assassin at 5 and 2, and he's got Derrick Henry and Omarion Hampton both in the lineup which is about as much downhill thunder as one roster should legally be allowed. Tim sits at 2 and 5 and needs a signature win badly, and he does have the horses to make noise with Josh Allen and Jahmyr Gibbs. History says blowouts are the theme here, with a 60.84 point margin logged just last year in week eight, somebody got run clean off the field. Tim's bench full of receivers, Worthy, Harrison Jr., Adonai Mitchell, is deep but his starting receiver corps of Parker Washington and DJ Moore feels thin by comparison, there might be an upgrade hiding in that pile. Still, Austin's the better, hungrier team right now. Give me Hampton Water.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) vs It might be #over (Drew)

Two teams at 2 and 5, both desperate to avoid staring down the barrel of a lost season, and the history is brutal for John S, who trails this series 1 to 3 with Drew having dropped a 59.38 point hammer back in 2024. John S has Josh Jacobs, Chase Brown, AND Jeremiyah Love all plugged into his lineup, which is an awful lot of running backs to be excited about, that's a strength. Drew counters with Lamar Jackson and Justin Jefferson up top, though I'll note Drew is sitting Cam Skattebo and Tyrone Tracy Jr. on the bench while starting Jordan Mason, which feels like it could bite him depending how the Giants backfield shakes out. Somebody's going to feel a little better about life after Thursday, er, after this week. I'll take It might be #over to extend their dominance in this series, narrowly.

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) vs Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah)

This is the marquee game of the week whether the league wants to admit it or not. Elijah's 6 and 1 and sitting atop the standings, the fallen founding king finally looking like a king again, while Matt R's 3 and 4 team is quietly the highest scoring team in the entire league at 747.84 points, which tells you this team has been getting unlucky, not untalented. The series is tied 2 and 2 with a 40.38 point margin set just last year. Elijah's lineup looks sharp top to bottom with Jonathan Taylor, Ashton Jeanty, CeeDee Lamb and Garrett Wilson, that's a murderer's row. Matt R meanwhile is sitting Emeka Egbuka on the bench behind Omar Cooper Jr. and Jaxon Smith Njigba, which I'm not sure I love, Egbuka's been a every week factor. Scoring says Matt R should win this, head to head history says it's a coin flip, I'll take the raw point total and ride with High Risk, High Reward to hand Elijah his first loss since forever.

JS N My Pants (Dallas) vs I still own you (John V)

The name of John V's team is about to become extremely ironic, because Dallas owns this series 3 to 0, including a 76.24 point annihilation last season, also in week nine, apparently week nine is when Dallas decides to end people's seasons early. Dallas sits at 5 and 2 with Joe Burrow and Saquon Barkley leading the charge, both lineups actually look clean this week, no real start sit crimes to report on either side, John V has got Jordan Love, Ja'Marr Chase and Brock Bowers rolling and that's a quality trio. But I can't bet against history this lopsided. Give me JS N My Pants to make it 4 and 0 in the rivalry and keep pace near the top of the standings.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: JS N My Pants over I still own you. Dallas has never lost this matchup and I see no reason that changes now.
  • Upset special: High Risk, High Reward over Sampson & Dylan-lila. Matt R's been the best scoring team in the league hiding behind a mediocre record, and Elijah's win streak has to end sometime.
  • Closest game to call: Serving Punt versus Matt's moms name, a genuine coin flip between two 4 and 3 clubs, complicated by Erica's curious quarterback choice.
