Week Thirteen: The Season Clings to Life, and So Do I

Gather close, degenerates, because Week Thirteen is the kind of week where the standings start getting honest with you whether you like it or not. Dallas sits at eight and three atop the mountain looking insufferable, Elijah's redemption tour keeps rolling at seven and four, and down in the basement I'm sitting here at four and seven writing this newsletter with the same steady hand I used to use writing championship victory speeches. Funny how that works. But forget my ledger for a second, because this week is loaded with measuring stick games, limping favorites, and at least one lineup decision that made me spit out my coffee. Let's get into it.

Mike'sBurnerAccount (Matt W) vs I still own you (John V)

Yes, I am required by law and personal pride to cover my own game first. I lead this series four to one, including a 49.2 point curb stomp back in 2024, and John V knows it, which is why he's been needling me in the group chat all week. My Kyren Williams and Jonah Coleman backfield duo gives me a solid floor, Malik Nabers is my swing for the fences, and I'll admit Chig Okonkwo at tight end is more prayer than plan. John V counters with Ja'Marr Chase and Breece Hall, which is about as scary a one two punch as this league has, and Jordan Love has quietly been palatable at quarterback. Here's my one gripe aimed at myself, because fairness matters even in my own write up: Tyreek Hill sitting on my bench while Ryan Flournoy and Konata Mumpfield start at wide receiver is the kind of thing that keeps a four and seven coach up at night. I'm not benching myself in print, but I'm thinking about it. Pick: me, because history says so and because I refuse to preview my own demise.

Mike Scream for Ice Cream (Ryan) vs Matt's moms name (Max)

Dead even series at three and three, and both of these teams are stuck at five and six staring up at the wild card picture like it's a cliff face. Ryan rolls out Patrick Mahomes and Christian McCaffrey, which on paper should be winning him more games than it has, but Luther Burden III and Antonio Williams at wideout isn't exactly striking fear into anybody. Max counters with Jayden Daniels and a four man rotation of running backs including Bijan Robinson and Alvin Kamara, which is as deep a backfield as you'll find in this league. I do wonder why Courtland Sutton is parked on Max's bench while Kyle Monangai, a running back, is starting at what amounts to a flex spot, but Max has clearly decided ground and pound is the identity here. Pick: Max, the backfield depth tips it.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs never been more #over (Drew)

Erica leads this series three to two and Drew is the proud owner of the league's worst record at three and eight, so on paper this should be a formality. But Drew's got Justin Jefferson going for him and Erica is trusting Deshaun Watson and Mike Gesicki, neither of whom scream confidence. Erica also has Justin Herbert rotting on her bench behind Watson, which feels like leaving a Ferrari in the garage to drive a riding mower, though I understand the matchup reasoning if Herbert's bye or matchup is ugly. Drew, meanwhile, is sitting Lamar Jackson in favor of Bo Nix, which is a stranger call given Lamar's ceiling, even on a bad Drew season. Pick: Erica, protecting her tiebreaker spot in a game that could get sloppy.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs How bout them Cowboys (John S)

Series tied at three apiece, but the records tell a different story this year. Austin's rolling at seven and four with Derrick Henry still pounding away and Omarion Hampton giving him a nasty one two punch at running back, plus George Kittle at tight end as a weekly luxury. John S is at three and eight and has Dak Prescott and Amon-Ra St. Brown doing their best to keep the lights on, but I notice Josh Jacobs buried on his bench while Justice Hill and Tyler Allgeier handle the backfield reps. That's a committee I wouldn't trust against a playoff contender. Pick: Austin, comfortably.

1-2-3 Packers! (Matt R) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

This is the nightmare matchup of the week for Matt R, who trails this series an almost comedic zero and five, with Dallas's widest margin of victory sitting at 45.22 points. Dallas is the league's best team at eight and three and brings Joe Burrow, Saquon Barkley, and a double tight end luxury package of Isaiah Likely and Sam LaPorta to the table. Matt R counters with Trevor Lawrence and Bucky Irving, and I'll note Emeka Egbuka sitting on his bench while Chris Olave and Ollie Gordon start feels like he's hoping a committee approach sneaks him a win against the hottest team in the league. Brave strategy. Doesn't usually work against Dallas. Pick: Dallas, the ghost of five straight head to head wins doesn't just disappear.

Will the Thrill (Tim) vs Corum Dei 💔 (Elijah)

Elijah's redemption arc, the one I've been tracking since he fell from 2016 champion to league doormat, continues to gain steam at seven and four, and he owns this series four to one over Tim with a 56.18 point blowout on the books. Elijah starts Jonathan Taylor and Ashton Jeanty together, which is about as nasty a running back duo as exists in fantasy right now, plus CeeDee Lamb outside. Tim fires back with Josh Allen and Jahmyr Gibbs, a strong core, but I have real questions about Jadarian Price getting run over Xavier Worthy and TreVeyon Henderson sitting cold on the bench. Worthy especially feels like a swing and miss if he's healthy. Pick: Elijah, the history and the backfield are both too loud to ignore.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Dallas over Matt R. Five straight head to head wins and the best record in the league speaks for itself.
  • Upset special: Drew over Erica. Justin Jefferson alone can erase a three and eight record for one Thursday to Monday stretch, and Erica's Watson led offense feels shakier than her record suggests.
  • Too close to call: Ryan versus Max. Two teams desperate to crawl back toward six and five, and both rosters have just enough questionable lineup choices to make it a coin flip.

The Back Page

Bold prediction, and I'm fully aware how this looks coming from the author, but somebody has to say it: Mike'sBurnerAccount climbs off the mat this week, beats John V to even our season series mismatch even further in my favor, and Tyreek Hill's continued benching becomes the moral victory nobody asked for but everyone will discuss in the group chat by Friday morning. Three championships don't write themselves into irrelevance just because of one rough autumn. The villain always has one more chapter left in him.
