Week Five: The Unbeaten, The Unproven, and One Shameless Homer Returns to Press Row

Gather close, degenerates, because Week Five is the week the league's tidy little record book starts getting messy. Hampton Water sits alone at four and zero like a man who found the last parking spot at the mall, and everybody else is circling, wondering when the regression fairy visits. Meanwhile I am two and two, which I will remind you is a .500 record carried by a roster that is one Kyren Williams heater away from looking like a juggernaut, and I am the one writing this, so draw your own conclusions about how generously that gets covered. Let's get to the matchups, because Thursday waits for no commissioner.

The Matchups

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs Serving Punt ✨ (Erica)

I lead this series four to one, and history says Erica's squad simply does not have my number, widest margin nineteen point three back in 2024. That said, I am not going to sit here and pretend my lineup is flawless just because I am the one typing. Dohnte Meyers in the flex over a bench stacked with Tyreek Hill, Josh Jacobs, and Travis Etienne is the kind of decision that makes a man's own beat reporter raise an eyebrow, even when that beat reporter is him. I will defend Kyren Williams and Puka Nacua to the grave, but somebody in my own war room needs to explain the Dohnte Meyers thing to me before kickoff. On the other side, Erica is trotting out Deshaun Watson and a running back room that includes both Aaron Jones and James Cook, which is fine, steady, unspectacular, the fantasy equivalent of oatmeal. I like my receivers, I like my history, and yes, I like myself in this one. Pick: Straight up Yorkin' it.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

Ryan owns this series three to zero, including a forty three point blowout in 2023, but none of that matters when Ryan is one and three and Dallas is sitting pretty at three and one. Here is the part that should worry Ryan's fan base, which I believe is just Ryan: Patrick Mahomes is sitting on his bench behind Dak Prescott. I understand loyalty to a starting quarterback, but when a former MVP is your QB2, you at least have to think about it. Dallas counters with Joe Burrow slinging it to Tee Higgins and Mike Evans, plus two tight ends in Sam LaPorta and Isaiah Likely, which is a nice problem to have. The history favors Ryan, the present favors Dallas, and I am siding with the team that is actually trending the right direction. Pick: JS N My Pants.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs High Risk, High Reward (Matt R)

Austin is the last unbeaten standing at four and zero, and he gets to do it against a team that trails him in the all-time series two to three despite Austin holding the better current record, fantasy football justice working in mysterious ways. Derrick Henry and Omarion Hampton give Austin a run game that should bully just about anybody, and Drake Maye slinging it to George Kittle and Jaylen Waddle rounds out a roster that frankly looks like it was built in a lab. Matt R counters with Christian McCaffrey and Ashton Jeanty, which is a dynamite one two punch when healthy, but Jared Goff at quarterback and a bench holding Emeka Egbuka unused while Michael Wilson starts is a choice I'd be squinting at if I were Matt R. The widest margin in this series was thirty five point eight eight back in 2024, and I would not be shocked if Austin flirts with that number again. Pick: Hampton Water.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) vs Will the Thrill (Tim)

This series is knotted at two and two, and both of these teams are stuck at the .500 line or worse trying to find separation. John S has Caleb Williams rotting on his bench behind Brock Purdy, which is a luxury problem but still worth a raised eyebrow given Purdy's volatility. Tyler Allgeier and Alvin Kamara in the same backfield for John S is a nice safety net regardless. Tim brings Josh Allen, which is the single best player in this game by a wide margin, but then parks Travis Kelce and a wideout room including Xavier Worthy and Marvin Harrison on the bench while starting Kalif Raymond, which feels like it is leaving points on the table. Allen is a great enough equalizer that I still lean his way, but barely, and it would not shock me if John S sneaks this one. Pick: Will the Thrill, in the closest game of the week.

Matt's moms name (Max) vs It might be #over (Drew)

Max owns this series three to zero, widest margin twenty two point two two in 2023, and Drew enters at one and three looking very much like a team whose nickname is doing a lot of foreshadowing. Max has a loaded bench, Jayden Daniels and DeVonta Smith sitting while C.J. Stroud and Courtland Sutton start, which is less a red flag and more a case of simply having too many good players, the kind of problem I wish I had. Drew's lineup leans on Bo Nix and a receiver room of Justin Jefferson, Brian Thomas, and Rome Odunze, which is talented enough to keep this interesting, but the history and the depth both point one way. Pick: Matt's moms name.

I still own you (John V) vs Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah)

John V trails this series two to three, including an absolutely cartoonish seventy three point margin back in 2024 that I assume is still talked about in whatever group chat John V lives in. Elijah is the hottest team in the league not named Hampton Water, three and one and second in total points at four hundred fifty five, and his lineup of Jalen Hurts, Jonathan Taylor, and CeeDee Lamb does not have a single glaring mistake on it. John V counters with Ja'Marr Chase and Brock Bowers, a strong top end, but Breece Hall sitting on his bench while Rico Dowdle starts is worth a second look given how banged up that Dallas backfield picture can get. Given Elijah's form and John V's history of getting run off the field in this exact matchup, I am siding with the hot hand. Pick: Sampson & Dylan-lila.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Hampton Water over High Risk, High Reward. Austin is four and zero for a reason and the roster gap here is the widest on the board.
  • Upset special: Will the Thrill over How bout them Cowboys. Josh Allen can single handedly erase a bad bench, and John S is leaving Caleb Williams on ice.
  • Too close to call: Straight up Yorkin' it versus Serving Punt, mostly because I refuse to bet against myself in print, but Dohnte Meyers is making me sweat.

The Back Page

Bold prediction: Straight up Yorkin' it survives its own questionable flex decision, Puka Nacua and Kyren Williams drag me to a win in spite of myself, and somewhere out there Hampton Water keeps rolling toward a five and zero start that is going to make the rest of this league very uncomfortable by Halloween. Remember, I have three rings and the best winning percentage in this league's history for a reason. Bet against me at your own peril.
