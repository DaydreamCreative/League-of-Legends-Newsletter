Week 8: The Joes Keep Winning, The Moms Keep Mourning

Gather round, degenerates, because Week 8 had a little bit of everything: a Burrow explosion that should come with a hazmat warning, a Battle of the Johns that lived up to its billing, and yours truly putting up a tidy little 63.38 that I will be discussing at length because, well, you know the deal by now. Let's get into it.

The Recaps

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) 63.38, It might be #over (Drew) 41.0

I'll keep the self congratulation to a professional minimum, but Puka Nacua's 19.5 and Kyren Williams' 12.4 did the heavy lifting for the good guys and I'll take the win, thank you very much. The real story here is Drew's continued war crimes against his own roster. The man started Lamar Jackson, who returned a flat zero, while Bo Nix sat on his bench posting 18.28. That is not a rounding error, that is a felony. Add in Chris Godwin Jr. also zeroing out in the lineup and you've got a team that basically forfeited two full roster spots before kickoff. I extend the series lead to 5 and 1, and I don't feel great about how I got there, but a win's a win and I didn't write the rules of fantasy football, I just report them with great relish.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) 117.38, Hampton Water (Austin) 76.16

Ryan absolutely teed off here, and the funny part is he did it without his best player. D'Andre Swift torched everybody for 26.9, Michael Wilson added a shocking 22.8, and Jonah Coleman chipped in 18.4, all while Patrick Mahomes sat on the bench and casually dropped 25.44 that nobody needed. That's the rare game where you bench a stud and still blow the doors off the building. Austin got a fine floor game from Drake Maye at 21.46, but Omarion Hampton's 3.7 was a gut punch and Tre Tucker's zero didn't help matters. The widest margin in series history, 41.22, and Ryan now leads it 4 to 1. Austin's going to need a minute after this one.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) 88.24, Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) 74.36

Erica got vintage-grade production from Jaylen Warren (19.8) and James Cook III (17.1), plus a sneaky 15.6 out of Mike Gesicki, and that was enough to survive a genuinely brutal process at the quarterback position, where Malik Willis somehow produced 14.64 and nobody is more surprised than me. Elijah countered with a monster 22.3 from Braelon Allen, but Jacoby Brissett's 3.46 under center was close to disqualifying, and leaving Jameson Williams (15.0) and DeMario Douglas (15.1) on the bench while Drake London mustered just 1.3 and Zay Flowers goose egged is the kind of roster math that keeps a first place team from pulling away. Erica climbs to 5 and 3 off this one and now owns some real momentum.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) 100.4, I still own you (John V) 112.12

The Battle of the Johns, round whatever we're on now, and John V took this one on the strength of an absolute Ja'Marr Chase eruption, 25.6, plus Brock Bowers chipping in 24.7 and Jordan Love quietly humming along at 21.52. John S actually had a fine lineup, Josh Jacobs went for 20.2 and Dak Prescott chipped in 19.8, but he's going to lose sleep over Tyler Allgeier sitting on his bench with 33.6 points while Jeremiyah Love limped to 2.3 in the lineup. That's a franchise altering amount of bench points left on the table, and it's also now the widest margin in the John S versus John V series at 11.72. John S still leads the overall series 3 to 2, but this one's going to leave a mark.

Matt's moms name (Max) 101.18, JS N My Pants (Dallas) 135.58

Dallas just happened to roster Joe Burrow this week, and Joe Burrow decided to go nuclear for 39.48 points, pairing it with a monstrous 32.5 from Tee Higgins. That's a Thursday night waiver wire miracle turning into a 135 point fireworks show, and Max simply had no answer, even with Bhayshul Tuten going off for a very nice 28.9. The frustrating part for Max has to be a bench full of zeroes, Kyle Monangai, Alvin Kamara, DeVonta Smith, Nico Collins, all goose eggs, so at least nothing got left behind, it just wasn't enough firepower to keep pace with a historic passing line. Dallas extends the series dominance to 4 and 0, and frankly at this point Max might want to just stop scheduling this matchup altogether.

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) 96.9, Will the Thrill (Tim) 113.96

Tim got the kind of quarterback performance you dream about, Josh Allen for 26.56, plus Jahmyr Gibbs (23.8) and Tony Pollard (26.2) both erupting in the same backfield, which is just unfair to everybody else in this league. Matt R fought hard, Bucky Irving went for 20.0 and Jaxon Smith-Njigba added 20.8, but the gut punch is Emmett Johnson sitting on the bench with 22.8 points while Omar Cooper Jr. zeroed out in the active lineup. That is a lineup decision that cost a very winnable game. The series is now knotted at 2 and 2, so at least there's a rubber match brewing somewhere down the line.

Standings and Movers

Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) and JS N My Pants (Dallas) remain glued to the top at 6 and 2 apiece, with Dallas's Burrow fireworks show keeping the pressure on. Behind them, Serving Punt (Erica) is the week's biggest climber, up two spots to third at 5 and 3, riding that gritty win over Elijah. Hampton Water (Austin) slides down one to fourth after that lopsided loss to Ryan, and I Still Own You (John V) jumps a spot to fifth thanks to the Battle of the Johns victory.

The biggest faller is Matt's Moms Name (Max), down two spots to sixth after the Burrow buzzsaw, now sitting 4 and 4 despite having the sixth highest point total in the entire league, proof that schedule luck is undefeated. My own Straight Up Yorkin' It climbs one spot to seventh after taking care of business against Drew, evening up to 4 and 4 myself. High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) drops one to eighth after the tough Tim loss, while Hawk Tua, Will the Thrill, How Bout Them Cowboys, and It Might Be #Over all held steady, for better or worse.

Looking Ahead

Week 9 has me drawing Hawk Tua on my Penix, fresh off that 117 point explosion, so wish me luck and maybe light a candle. Elsewhere, Dallas and John V collide in a legitimate top five showdown, Elijah has to deal with a desperate Matt R squad, and the Cowboys and #Over tilt is basically a play in game for who gets to stop being bad together.
