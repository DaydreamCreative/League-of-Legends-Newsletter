Week Seven: The GOAT Tries to Remember What Winning Feels Like

Gather round, legends, because Week Seven arrives like a plot twist nobody asked for: the two unbeaten juggernauts of this league, JS N My Pants and Sampson & Dylan-lila, finally collide at five and one apiece, while yours truly sits at two and four trying to climb out of a hole I dug with my own two thumbs on waivers. I am not going to pretend objectivity here. I never have. But even a biased man can see the real story of Thursday night through Monday: this is the week the top of the standings eats itself, and the middle class gets a chance to sneak up on everybody while they're distracted. Let's get into it.

The Matchups

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs Hampton Water (Austin)

I trail this series one to two, and Austin's crew comes in five and one, riding Derrick Henry, Omarion Hampton, and a suddenly dangerous Drake Maye throwing it to Jaylen Waddle and George Pickens. That is a loaded roster, and I will say it plainly because self pity does not win football games. But here is why I like my spot anyway: Puka Nacua is a problem for any secondary, Josh Jacobs is a bellcow I am thrilled to roll out, and Stafford to Juwan Johnson is a sneaky touchdown source nobody in this league is accounting for. My one gripe, and I am allowed to gripe about my own team because that is the whole bit, is Roschon Johnson getting a starting nod over a healthy Travis Etienne Jr. sitting on my bench. Etienne is the better bet in a flex spot most weeks, and if I lose this by a field goal I am going to be insufferable about it to myself in the mirror. Austin's lineup, meanwhile, looks clean top to bottom, no real sins to confess there, which is the most annoying thing about a five and one team. History says blowouts are possible in this series, the widest margin a brutal 31.02 back in 2023. I am picking my own team here, obviously, because what did you expect, but I will admit it is a coin flip dressed up as confidence.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) vs High Risk, High Reward (Matt R)

Two and four meets two and four, and somebody here finally gets to feel good about themselves again. Ryan trails this head to head one and three with a 52.12 beatdown in the scar tissue from 2023, but this year's roster has real juice, Caleb Williams and Christian McCaffrey running the show with Malik Nabers and Rome Odunze outside. The wrinkle worth yelling about is Patrick Mahomes rotting on Ryan's bench behind Caleb Williams. I understand the weekly matchup logic, but benching a former MVP quarterback is always a declaration, and I want it on record that I noticed. On the other side, Matt R is leaning on Michael Penix Jr. and Bucky Irving, with Jaxon Smith-Njigba as the headline receiver, but Emeka Egbuka watching from the bench behind Omar Cooper Jr. and Colston Loveland raises an eyebrow in my newsroom. This one is close enough that I will call it for Ryan at home, mostly because I think Caleb Williams's weapons are deeper than Matt R's right now.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs Will the Thrill (Tim)

Erica sits three and three and leads this series two games to two, with a 15.18 margin as the high water mark, which tells you these two have mostly played tight, nervy affairs. Deshaun Watson slinging it to Drake London and Rashee Rice gives Erica real firepower, and Jaylen Warren plus Aaron Jones Sr. is a sturdy two headed backfield. Tim counters with Jahmyr Gibbs, which is close to a cheat code at running back, plus Kyler Murray and a double tight end look with Travis Kelce and Michael Mayer that is either genius or a wasted roster spot depending on the week. Worth flagging: Tim has Josh Allen parked on his bench, which is the kind of quarterback insurance most of this league would kill for and he is choosing not to use it. That is either supreme confidence in Murray or an oversight waiting to bite him. I like Gibbs to be the best player on the field Thursday into Sunday, and I will take Tim in a mild upset of the series trend.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) vs Matt's moms name (Max)

John S leads this series two to one but sits two and four this season, leaning on Brock Purdy, Jeremiyah Love, and the Amon Ra St. Brown and A.J. Brown receiver tandem that should be unfair on paper. Max counters at three and three with C.J. Stroud, Bijan Robinson, and a very solid Nico Collins and DeVonta Smith one two punch. Here is my question for the John S household: Dak Prescott is sitting on the bench behind Brock Purdy, and while I understand Purdy has been fine, Dak has the higher ceiling most weeks, and in a game John S probably needs a shootout to win, I would want my higher variance arm in the lineup. Max's lineup looks tidier, with Jayden Daniels reasonably benched behind a healthy Stroud. I will take Max to even this series up.

It might be #over (Drew) vs I still own you (John V)

Drew trails this series one to two and comes in two and four, but Lamar Jackson and Justin Jefferson give him a top shelf top of the roster no matter the record. The concerning part of Drew's lineup is Brian Thomas Jr. stapled to the bench while Jalen Coker and Chris Godwin Jr. start at wide receiver. Thomas has the kind of weekly ceiling that should not be an afterthought, and I think that is the single most fixable start sit error on the board this week. John V, three and three, counters with Jordan Love feeding Ja'Marr Chase and a trio of complementary backs in Quinshon Judkins and Breece Hall. That backfield duo alone should carry him here. I am picking John V, and I am mentioning the Brian Thomas situation one more time on my way out the door because Drew needs to hear it twice.

JS N My Pants (Dallas) vs Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah)

The marquee game of the week, two five and one teams, and Dallas has lost every single meeting in this series, zero and three, including a 47.74 annihilation last season that I assume still gives him night sweats. Joe Burrow, Saquon Barkley, and Tee Higgins make Dallas a legitimate buzzsaw this year regardless of history, and Sam LaPorta at tight end is pure gravy. Elijah counters with Jonathan Taylor and Ashton Jeanty, a running back room that might be the best pure volume duo in the entire league, plus CeeDee Lamb and Garrett Wilson outside. Both lineups look sharp, no bench regrets jumping off the page for either side, which almost never happens in this column. Given Elijah's perfect record in this series and a monster points total already banked this season, I have to lean Sampson & Dylan Lila, but I would not be shocked if Dallas finally breaks through. This is the one to circle.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Sampson & Dylan Lila's running back room should carry Elijah through almost any matchup, series history included.
  • Upset special: Max over How bout them Cowboys, on the strength of a cleaner lineup and the Dak Prescott question mark haunting John S.
  • Too close to call: JS N My Pants versus Sampson & Dylan Lila, two unbeaten contenders with no obvious lineup edge either way.
