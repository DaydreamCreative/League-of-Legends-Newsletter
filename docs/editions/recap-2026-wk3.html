By Matthew Waters

Week Three: The Purdy Explosion, The Bijan Massacre, and the Great Yorkin Ascension

Gather round, degenerates, because Week Three had a little bit of everything: a bench quarterback throwing for 31 points while getting benched anyway (more on that crime scene shortly), a 34 point Bijan Robinson evening that still wasn't enough, and yours truly quietly climbing the standings like the dynasty landlord I am. I'm typing this section with a completely straight face. Straight up Yorkin' it improved to two and one and jumped five spots, and yes, I already know how that reads coming from the guy writing the newsletter. I contain multitudes. Let's get into it.

The Recaps

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) 95.1, How bout them Cowboys (John S) 87.78

I'll try to be humble about this one, I really will. Matthew Stafford quietly put up 20.9, Kyren Williams turned in 18.8, and Davante Adams chipped in 17.2 to give me a nice, boring, professional win. The only blemish was Quentin Johnston no showing at 5.5 and Terrance Ferguson doing next to nothing at 1.4, but championship teams win ugly sometimes. Meanwhile John S got an absolutely absurd 31.28 from Brock Purdy, the kind of start that should win you a week on its own, and it still wasn't enough because the skill positions around him fell asleep. Amon-Ra St. Brown managed only 9.9, Ladd McConkey 8.6, and Chase Brown was a non factor at 7.9. The dagger though was John S sitting Rachaad White, who put up 10.6 on the bench, in favor of a Chase Brown who couldn't crack single digit relevance. Small margins, John. Small margins. History now favors me three games to two in this series, and I intend to keep it that way, objectively and without bias, obviously.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) 87.7, I still own you (John V) 115.58

John V lived up to the team name here. Brock Bowers went off for 22.6, Ja'Marr Chase delivered 20.3, and Matthew Golden chipped in 18.5, giving John V a balanced beatdown despite Jordan Love only managing 17.48. Ryan got a monster night from Jaxon Smith Njigba, who dropped 30.36 and nearly saved this one single handedly, plus a nice 16.3 from Luther Burden. But Patrick Mahomes only mustered 15.94, and the real gut punch was Ryan leaving Dak Prescott's 18.94 on the bench in favor of that Mahomes line. When your bench quarterback outscores your started quarterback by three points, that's not variance, that's a lineup decision that needs to answer for itself. The series stays lopsided, with John V now up four games to two.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) 115.84, High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) 96.06

Erica's receivers just ate. Drake London put up 23.9, Jaylen Warren ran for 19.1, and James Cook chipped in 18.4, giving her a genuinely dominant offensive line up top to bottom. The one sour note was leaving Harold Fannin's 20.6 on the bench at tight end, which is the kind of thing that would sting more if she hadn't won by nearly twenty points anyway. Matt R got a huge surprise night from Michael Wilson at 20.4 and a strong 19.6 from Christian McCaffrey, but Malik Nabers cratered at 5.1 and Emeka Egbuka was a non factor at 8.7, and those receiver rooms just couldn't keep pace. Matt R also had Trevor Lawrence's 19.78 sitting on his bench behind Jared Goff's 19.36, basically a wash, so that one's forgivable. Matt R drops to zero and three, and it's officially time to panic in that camp.

Hampton Water (Austin) 94.46, JS N My Pants (Dallas) 82.28

George Kittle continues to be a menace, putting up 23.2, and Christian Watson chipped in a surprising 19.1 to carry Austin despite Drake Maye posting a genuinely horrifying 1.76 at quarterback. That's not a typo. One point seven six. And Austin still won by twelve because Derrick Henry ran for 21.4 like a man with something to prove. On the other side, Dallas got a strong 21.58 from Joe Burrow and 18.0 from Tee Higgins, but De'Von Achane was a complete ghost at 1.7 and Tetairoa McMillan barely registered at 2.7. The killer for Dallas was Sam Darnold sitting on the bench with 27.66 points while Joe Burrow started instead, a totally defensible call in theory that happened to cost him the game in practice. Austin extends the series lead to three games to two and stays undefeated at three and zero.

Matt's moms name (Max) 93.98, Will the Thrill (Tim) 125.86

Bijan Robinson had one of those nights, 34.3 points, an absolute masterpiece, and Max still lost by over thirty. That's how you know you're up against it. Bhayshul Tuten pitched in 16.0, but Nico Collins was an absolute zero and Denzel Boston mustered only 7.1, and the supporting cast just could not hold up its end. Meanwhile Tim got an even more ridiculous 37.9 out of Jahmyr Gibbs and 19.4 from Jeremiyah Love, giving him back to back monster running backs that no roster on earth was built to withstand. Josh Allen was merely fine at 15.96, and Xavier Worthy stunk it up at 3.0, but none of it mattered next to that backfield. Max also had Jordan Addison's 17.5 rotting on the bench, which would have helped plenty but wouldn't have flipped this one. The series now sits at an almost cruel five and zero in Tim's favor. Somebody check on Max.

Real Eaters (Drew) 81.94, Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) 94.02

Elijah's Garrett Wilson exploded for 21.7 and CeeDee Lamb chipped in 16.7, and Jalen Hurts did just enough at 12.62 to keep the unbeaten machine running. Drew actually got a great story going, Juwan Johnson at tight end randomly dropping 18.3, plus 20.44 from Lamar Jackson and 20.3 from Kenneth Walker, and it still wasn't enough because Justin Jefferson had an uncharacteristic dud at 4.2 and Chris Godwin matched him at 4.0. Two ice cold wide receiver weeks from stars will sink you every time. Elijah stretches the series edge to three games to one and, more importantly, keeps his club spotless at three and zero.

Standings and Movers

Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) sits at the top at three and zero with 340.9 points, holding steady at number one, with Hampton Water (Austin) right behind also at three and zero. After that it gets interesting. I'm proud, and only a little embarrassed to report, that Straight up Yorkin' it climbed five whole spots into third place at two and one. Serving Punt (Erica) also jumped five spots into fourth, riding that 115.84 explosion. JS N My Pants (Dallas) slid down two spots after the Drake Maye disaster game. Down in the basement, Will the Thrill (Tim) actually rose four spots despite sitting at one and two, because 340.94 points on the season says he's been getting unlucky more than bad, while How bout them Cowboys, Real Eaters, Hawk Tua on my Penix, and Matt's moms name all fell three spots apiece in a pileup of misery. High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) remains winless at zero and three and dropped another spot, which at this point is less a slump and more a crisis of faith.

Looking Ahead

Week four brings me face to face with JS N My Pants in a battle of two and one clubs, and I like my chances given recent history. Elsewhere, the Battle of the Johns lies dormant this week but Ryan and John S square off in a battle of the desperate at one and two apiece, Erica gets a real test against unbeaten Hampton Water, and poor Max draws the unbeaten juggernaut Sampson & Dylan-lila, which feels like piling on at this point. Real Eaters gets to feast on the winless High Risk, High Reward, and Will the Thrill hosts John V in a clash of two teams that are better than their records suggest.

The Back Page

I want to be very clear that I am a neutral, unbiased chronicler of this league's events, and it is a total coincidence that the week I'm writing up happens to be the week I jumped five spots in the standings on the back of a clean 95.1 team win. Three rings, best win percentage in league history, and now apparently ghostwriting my own hype in the third person disguised as journalism. Somebody stop me. Actually, don't. See everyone in Week Four.
