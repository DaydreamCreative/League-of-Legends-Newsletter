Week Three: The Week the Undefeateds Kept Rolling and My Bench Nearly Committed Fraud

Gather close, degenerates, because Week Three delivered a little bit of everything: a Brock Purdy fireworks show that still lost, a Bijan Robinson masterpiece that also lost, and yours truly sneaking out of Dallas with a win despite leaving Tyler Shough's 21.8 points to rot on my bench like leftovers nobody wants to admit they made. Sampson & Dylan-lila and Hampton Water remain the league's two unbeaten juggernauts, sitting pretty at three and zero while the rest of us claw around in the muck. Let's get into it.

The Recaps

Straight up Yorkin' it 95.1, How bout them Cowboys 87.78

I'll take the win, but let's not pretend I did this cleanly. Matthew Stafford gave me a workmanlike 20.9, Kyren Williams bulldozed his way to 18.8, and Davante Adams chipped in 17.2 to keep the ship afloat. But I benched Tyler Shough's 21.8 points in favor of Stafford, which means I essentially left a touchdown on the table and won anyway, a level of managerial genius I refuse to apologize for. Meanwhile John Sagherian got an absolute masterpiece from Brock Purdy, 31.28 points worth of masterpiece, and it still wasn't enough because the rest of his lineup no showed. Chase Brown managed a measly 7.9, Amon Ra St. Brown limped to 9.9, and Ladd McConkey turned in 8.6. When your quarterback outscores three of your skill players combined, you're not winning that game. History now sits 3 and 2 in my favor, and John S continues to search for the formula that turns one great arm into a whole functioning offense.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ 87.7, I still own you 115.58

Jaxon Smith Njigba turned in a video game number, 30.36 points, and Ryan still lost by nearly 28. That is the kind of stat line that makes a man question the universe. The rest of Ryan's lineup simply didn't show up to help him, Patrick Mahomes managed only 15.94, and Kyle Pitts contributed basically nothing at 1.0. Meanwhile John Vaughan got balanced production across the board, Ja'Marr Chase for 20.3, Matthew Golden for 18.5, and Brock Bowers going off for 22.6, and that's how you win a game without needing a single Herculean effort. John V improves to 1 and 2 and can credit the tight end position for doing the heavy lifting.

Serving Punt ✨ 115.84, High Risk High Reward 96.06

Erica rolled into this one and made it look easy, Drake London exploding for 23.9, Jaylen Warren chipping in a shockingly good 19.1, and James Cook adding 18.4. That's three players over eighteen points, which is the kind of depth that wins leagues. Matt R got a nice night from Michael Wilson at 20.4 and Christian McCaffrey chugged along at 19.6, but he left Trevor Lawrence's 19.78 on his bench in favor of Jared Goff's 19.36, basically a wash, so that one's not the crime. The real issue was Malik Nabers cratering at 5.1 and Emeka Egbuka doing next to nothing at 8.7. Matt R drops to 0 and 3, and the vibes in that draft room are starting to sour.

Hampton Water 94.46, JS N My Pants 82.28

Austin somehow won this despite getting 1.76 points out of Drake Maye, which should be studied by scientists. George Kittle bailed him out with 23.2, Christian Watson chipped in a lovely 19.1, and Derrick Henry ground out 21.4. That is one of the more entertaining "my quarterback basically didn't play" wins you'll see all year. Meanwhile Dallas got a strong 21.58 from Joe Burrow but sat Sam Darnold's 27.66 on the bench, which looking back is a decision he's going to be thinking about at three in the morning for a while. De'Von Achane also completely disappeared for 1.7 points, and that kind of thing will sink you against a team getting 23 points out of its tight end. Hampton Water improves to 3 and 0 and remains the league's most improbable machine.

Matt's moms name 93.98, Will the Thrill 125.86

Bijan Robinson went off for 34.3 points for Max, and somehow it wasn't the best running back performance of the night, because Jahmyr Gibbs dropped 37.9 for Tim. That's two backs combining for over seventy points in one matchup, a genuinely absurd football event. Max also got 16.0 out of Bhayshul Tuten but Nico Collins completely no showed with a zero, and that's the difference in a game decided by about thirty two points. Tim rode Gibbs, a strong 19.4 from Jeremiyah Love, and a competent floor game everywhere else to run his rivalry record over Max to a perfect five and oh. Max's moms name remains winless against Will the Thrill since the dawn of recorded history, and at this point it's less a rivalry than a recurring nightmare.

Real Eaters 81.94, Sampson & Dylan-lila 94.02

Drew got a strong 20.44 from Lamar Jackson and 20.3 from Kenneth Walker, plus an out of nowhere 18.3 from Juwan Johnson, but Justin Jefferson completely vanished for 4.2 points and that's simply not allowed when you're paying superstar prices. Elijah didn't have a single monster performance, Garrett Wilson led the way at 21.7, but he got contributions across the board and that consistency is exactly how you go three and oh. Sampson & Dylan-lila remains the last unbeaten standing atop the mountain, and Drew falls to 1 and 2 still searching for the version of Jefferson that shows up on Sundays.

Standings and Movers

Sampson & Dylan-lila sits at three and oh atop the standings with 340.9 points, followed closely by Hampton Water also unbeaten at three and oh. I'm sitting pretty at third with a two and one record, up five spots after that win in Dallas, right alongside Serving Punt who also jumped five spots into fourth. JS N My Pants slid down two spots to fifth after that loss to Hampton Water. The big fallers this week were brutal: How bout them Cowboys dropped three spots despite Purdy's monster game, Real Eaters fell three spots after the Jefferson no show, and Hawk Tua on my Penix dropped three spots as well after getting outscored despite Smith Njigba's huge night. Will the Thrill jumped four spots on the strength of that Gibbs explosion, and Matt's moms name continued its freefall, now down three spots and still searching for anything against Tim.

Looking Ahead

Week Four brings a genuine measuring stick match up as I take on JS N My Pants with both of us sitting at two and one, plus the Battle of the Johns reignites when Hawk Tua on my Penix hosts How bout them Cowboys. Serving Punt gets a real test against unbeaten Hampton Water, and poor Matt's moms name draws unbeaten Sampson & Dylan-lila, which feels like scheduling malpractice. Elsewhere, Real Eaters looks to kick a struggling High Risk High Reward squad while they're down, and Will the Thrill hosts I still own you in a battle of two and two hopefuls trying to find their footing.

The Back Page

Look, I know how this sounds coming from the guy writing the newsletter, but benching a 21.8 point quarterback and still winning by more than seven points is not luck, it's a demonstration of sheer roster depth and generational football intelligence. Some of you are out there sitting Sam Darnold's 27.66 like it's radioactive, and I'm out here proving that even my mistakes work out. Three titles doesn't happen by accident, folks. It happens by accident, actually, and then you write the newsletter about it yourself. See everyone next week.
