Week Ten: The Week I Became My Own Cautionary Tale

Let me get this out of the way while I still have some dignity left in the tank. I, Matthew Waters, three time champion, owner of the best winning percentage in the history of this league, started Malik Nabers for zero points and benched a man named Seth McGowan who dropped thirteen point seven on me from the pine. That is not a typo. That is not a joke I wrote for flavor. That happened in real football, on purpose, with my own two thumbs on the lineup screen. I will cover the rest of this week's carnage with my usual journalistic integrity, but know that I am writing through my own tears, and know that my bias detector is running on fumes because frankly I do not deserve any home cooking this week.

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) 58.76 vs 147.34 Will the Thrill (Tim)

I will not bury the lede on my own disaster. Fifty eight point seven six points is the kind of number you post when you forget the games started. Ryan Flournoy quietly gave me sixteen point one, Stafford put up a respectable nineteen point seven six, but then the rest of my lineup simply did not show up. Kyren Williams managed four point two, Dylan Sampson five point seven, A.J. Brown a ghostly three point seven, and Malik Nabers, again, zero. Meanwhile I had Seth McGowan sitting on my bench making thirteen point seven points of eye contact with me the entire night. On the other sideline, Tim's Will the Thrill outfit put on an absolute clinic, with Jahmyr Gibbs going nuclear for forty point seven and Parker Washington chipping in twenty four point seven almost as an afterthought. Josh Allen tossed in twenty two point three four, Tony Pollard added nineteen point nine, and even Tim's bench was loaded with stuff he didn't need. This one was never within shouting distance, and the series now sits at Tim leading one to four, with this the new widest margin in franchise history between these two clubs. I have nothing else to say about this game. I am going to go stand outside for a while.

F*q it we hibernate 🧸 (Ryan) 118.12 vs 98.0 It might be #over (Drew)

Ryan's Christian McCaffrey decided to have one of those weeks, dropping forty four point three points like he was trying to make a statement to the rest of the league, and Patrick Mahomes backed him up with thirty five point four two. That is a nearly eighty point one two combo from just two roster spots, which is the kind of thing that makes the rest of us want to quit and take up disc golf. Drew got a nice floor from Cam Skattebo at twenty five point seven and Deebo Samuel at twenty four point four, but Jalen Coker's zero point three and a shaky Drew Lock game at the quarterback spot left too big a hole to climb out of. Ryan's bench, notably, had Malik Nabers sitting there scoring three point nine, so no harm no foul, but it is worth noting Drew left Keenan Allen's eight point six on the bench in a game decided by twenty points, which stings a little extra in hindsight.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) 99.08 vs 97.32 I still own you (John V)

This one came down to the wire and honestly deserved a bigger stage. Erica got balanced production everywhere, James Cook going for twenty one point seven, Deshaun Watson chipping in twenty point three eight, and a sneaky twelve point six from Harold Fannin at tight end that ended up being the difference. John V had Ja'Marr Chase go off for twenty seven point six, which on most nights wins you the game outright, but Germie Bernard's one point zero and Brock Bowers managing only two point eight at tight end left too much on the table. The real gut punch for John V is what was sitting on his bench: Josh Downs quietly put up twelve point three and Jordan Love ten point eight, numbers that would have flipped this result had either been in the lineup. Erica moves to three and two in this head to head series and keeps her season afloat.

Hampton Water (Austin) 97.52 vs 76.22 Lamb to the Slaughter (Elijah)

George Pickens turned in the single best fantasy performance of the entire week among all twelve teams, thirty eight points on the nose, and it single handedly bailed out an Austin lineup that got nothing from his quarterback. Drake Maye finished with three point zero two, which is a number you usually see from a kicker having a bad day, but between Pickens, Derrick Henry's sixteen point five, and Omarion Hampton's sixteen point zero, Austin had enough cushion to survive. Elijah got a tidy twenty point four two from Jared Goff and sixteen point seven from CeeDee Lamb, but Tyler Warren's one point four and DeMario Douglas's zero point nine were quiet killers, and Jordyn Tyson's thirteen point five sat untouched on his bench while his lineup sputtered. Hampton Water climbs a spot in the standings on the strength of one man's monster Sunday.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) 92.66 vs 135.16 JS N My Pants (Dallas)

The irony here is thick enough to cut with a knife: John S got thirty one point six six from Brock Purdy and still left his actual best option, Dak Prescott's thirty four point four two, rotting on the bench. That is nearly thirty five points of pure math that simply never got to count, in a game decided by over forty. Amon Ra St. Brown chipped in a strong twenty three point eight for John S, but it was nowhere near enough against a Dallas squad getting thirty three point one six from Joe Burrow, thirty one point seven from Chuba Hubbard, and a shockingly good twenty two point two from Isaiah Likely at tight end. Dallas extends the all time series lead to five to one and keeps pace atop the league standings.

Matt's moms name (Max) 104.86 vs 126.18 High Risk, High Reward (Matt R)

Matt R got a borderline absurd thirty one point one out of Emmett Johnson, a number most starting running backs would sign up for in their sleep, paired with twenty seven point one from Jaxon Smith-Njigba and twenty six point seven eight from Trevor Lawrence. That is simply too much firepower for Max to handle even with a strong, balanced day of his own, Jayden Daniels at twenty point seven six, Nico Collins at nineteen point nine, and Bhayshul Tuten chipping in eighteen point eight. The Omar Cooper zero on Matt R's side barely mattered with that much production elsewhere. Max falls a brutal five spots in the standings this week despite scoring over a hundred points, proof that in this league you can play well and still get run over.

Standings and Movers

JS N My Pants (Dallas) sits comfortably at the top at seven and three with 1075.32 points, holding firm at number one. Behind them it is Lamb to the Slaughter (Elijah) at six and four, I still own you (John V) also six and four, both steady with no movement. Hampton Water (Austin) and Serving Punt (Erica) both climbed a spot to four and five in the table thanks to their wins, each sitting at six and four. High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) rode the Emmett Johnson explosion up a spot to five and five, and F*q it we hibernate (Ryan) climbed as well off that McCaffrey and Mahomes combo, also five and five. Will the Thrill (Tim) is this week's big riser, up two full spots after dropping a hundred forty seven point three four bomb on yours truly, now five and five. The biggest faller by a mile is Matt's moms name (Max), tumbling five spots despite a hundred four point eight six points, a cruel reminder that good games mean nothing if the other guy has a better one. And then there is me, Straight up Yorkin' it, down a spot to four and six, which honestly might be generous given the Malik Nabers situation I am still processing.

Looking Ahead

Week eleven brings me face to face with Lamb to the Slaughter (Elijah), and frankly after this week I deserve whatever is coming. Ryan and Tim run it back in a battle of five and five squads looking to climb further, Erica gets a very winnable date with the struggling How bout them Cowboys, Austin and Max collide in a measuring stick game, Drew tries to play spoiler against league leading Dallas, and Matt R hosts John V in a matchup between two teams very much alive in the chase.

The Back Page

I want to be very clear that I am a three time champion, the winningest manager in the history of this league, and a man of considerable football judgment, and this week I benched a running back for thirteen point seven points in favor of a wide receiver who put up a doughnut. I will be reviewing my own lineup decisions with the same scrutiny I apply to everyone else, which is to say mercilessly and in print, forever. See everyone next week, when I promise to at least check the injury report before kickoff.
