Zero Hour: The 2026 Slate Opens With Every Team At Nothing And Nothing

Gather round, degenerates. The record book flips to a fresh page, every team in the league sits at zero and zero, and for one shining week we all get to pretend we have a chance. I say pretend because, as the reigning historian and unbiased chronicler of this operation, I feel obligated to remind everyone that Matt Waters has three rings sitting on a shelf and a career win percentage that makes the rest of the field look like they are playing a different sport. That's not bragging. That's just what the record book says, and I only report what I see, even when what I see is my own greatness staring back at me.

Still, zero and zero is the great equalizer. Somewhere in this league of twelve, a dynasty is either about to extend or finally about to crack. Somewhere a ghost of 2016 stirs. Somewhere the Battle of the Johns quietly reloads. I don't know the schedule grid yet, so consider this week's dispatch less a box score and more a scouting report on every soul brave enough to log in for another year of pain.

Around the League

Straight Up Yorkin' It

Sitting pretty at the top of the standings by virtue of alphabetical order and absolutely nothing else, Straight Up Yorkin' It opens the year with a clean slate and a name that promises chaos. No history to lean on yet this season, which in this league usually means either a sleeper title run or an early bye week from relevance. I'd watch this one just to see which it is.

Good Old American Values

A patriotic entry that has never once, to my knowledge, displayed any actual values, good, old, or American. That's fine. This league was built on false advertising. The name alone deserves a wild card berth in the power rankings conversation, if nothing else.

Serving Punt

Bold to name your team after the single worst outcome in football, but I respect the honesty. If this team lives up to its own branding, expect a lot of fourth and long decisions all season. If it doesn't, we have a sneaky sleeper on our hands.

Fitzmagic

Named for the patron saint of chaotic quarterback play, and if there's one thing this league loves, it's chaos. Fitzmagic teams have a habit of looking either brilliant or disastrous with no in between. Week one is the calm before that particular storm.

Team Sagherian

John Sagherian's crew, and longtime readers know exactly what that name conjures: the Battle of the Johns, 2020 edition, when John S went nine and four, John Vaughan somehow won the whole thing at eight and five, and Matt Waters posted the best record in the league at eleven and two and still went home empty handed. Yes, I bring that up unprompted every year. No, I will not stop. John S has been chasing that ghost ever since, and 2026 is another chance to finally get the Johns saga a happy ending that isn't mine.

Matt's Moms Name

Now here is a squad I happen to know quite intimately, seeing as it is mine. Three championships, 2019, 2021, and 2023, an all time win percentage nobody else in this league can touch, and a single week scoring record of two hundred five point one eight that has stood since 2019 and will likely outlive us all. I promise to cover this team with total journalistic neutrality, right after I finish reminding you of all that.

Real Eaters

A team name that suggests appetite, and in fantasy football appetite usually means somebody is about to overspend on waivers by week three. No prior data to lean on, which around here just means the mystery is part of the charm.

Max's Moms Name

Max Henry's entry, and per the record book Max carries a career win percentage of four hundred twenty nine, solidly in the lower half of the active field. Not a dynasty, not a disaster, just a steady presence that occasionally sneaks up on somebody. This year could be the year he flips the script, or it could be more of the same respectable mediocrity.

Will The Thrill

A name that promises excitement it has yet to be forced to deliver on, since the season hasn't kicked off. I'll allow the marketing department some good faith for one more week before I start asking for receipts.
