Week 11 Recap, 2026November 25, 2026
By Matthew Waters

Week Eleven: The Villain Takes an Arrow While the Rest of the League Burns Pretty, Pretty Pictures

I'm going to be professional about this. I'm going to report the news of Week Eleven with the same clear eyed objectivity I bring every Tuesday, and I am absolutely not going to spend three paragraphs sulking about the fact that Straight up Yorkin' it dropped another one, this time 87.08 to 102.06 to Lamb to the Slaughter, while Jonah Coleman went off for 32.9 points on my own bench spending roster and I still found a way to lose. No sulking. Just the facts. Meanwhile, across the league, Dak Prescott decided to cosplay as peak Dan Marino, Ashton Jeanty kept auditioning for the "guy who ruins your week" role, and poor Drew Knight posted a number so low I had to read it twice. Let's get into it.

The Recaps

Lamb to the Slaughter 102.06, Straight up Yorkin' it 87.08

Fine. Let's talk about my week, since apparently I have to. Jonah Coleman was an absolute monster for me with 32.9 points, and Malik Nabers chipped in a respectable 15.0, but everything after that fell off a cliff. Seth McGowan managed only 1.3, Ryan Flournoy gave me 1.6, and I started Anthony Gould's 5.8 over a Tre' Harris who sat on my bench doing nothing at all with 3.8, which in hindsight did not matter but still bugs me. On the other side, Elijah got the kind of balanced night that wins these things, with Ashton Jeanty exploding for 25.4 and CeeDee Lamb adding 24.7, and Jared Goff quietly throwing in 20.36 at quarterback. Jonathan Taylor was the one cold spot at 4.7, but Elijah had more than enough thunder elsewhere. The series stays close at Elijah leading 3 games to 2, and I'm already circling the rematch.

Will the Thrill 109.66, F*q it we hibernate 🧸 95.76

Tim's title defense of his own roster decisions continues, this time on the back of Jahmyr Gibbs going for 25.8 and Travis Kelce turning back the clock for 22.4, with DJ Moore adding a tidy 19.1. Josh Allen was oddly human at just 12.16, but Tim didn't need him. Ryan countered with Patrick Mahomes putting up 24.96 and Javonte Williams chipping in 22.6, but the roster math betrayed him. Ryan left Antonio Williams on the bench with 16.7 points doing absolutely nothing while Rome Odunze (6.5) and Luther Burden III (2.3) stunk up the starting lineup, and that gap is almost exactly the margin of defeat. Painful bench management, Ryan. Painful.

How bout them Cowboys 92.2, Serving Punt ✨ 83.24

Dak Prescott threw for a video game worthy 35.9 points and John Sagherian got enough help from Ladd McConkey's 17.2 and Tetairoa McMillan's 16.1 to hold off a game Erica squad. Erica got a strong 21.44 from Justin Herbert and 16.2 from James Cook III, but J.K. Dobbins contributed a flat zero and Jaylen Warren limped to 3.9, and the math never quite caught up. Worth noting John S left Brock Purdy's 22.2 on his own bench, which is the kind of thing you can get away with when your starting quarterback drops 35.9 anyway.

Hampton Water 95.76, Matt's moms name 75.36

Austin Hermann rolled with George Pickens for 20.3 and George Kittle for 16.9, got a credible floor game from Bryce Young at 15.96, and cruised. Max got a fine 17.06 from Jayden Daniels but then watched Kyle Monangai turn in a ghostly 1.8, and the supporting cast of Khalil Shakir (12.8) and Nico Collins (13.3) just wasn't enough firepower to keep pace. Max also had C.J. Stroud sitting there with 20.46 points doing nothing on the bench while Jayden Daniels started instead, a decision that in retrospect cost more than a touchdown's worth of regret.

JS N My Pants 108.8, never been more #over 50.4

This one got ugly fast. Chuba Hubbard erupted for 26.0 points and Mike Evans added 19.9 as Dallas rolled to the week's most lopsided laugher. Drew's lineup, meanwhile, simply had nowhere to hide, with Bo Nix managing 11.5, Cam Skattebo scraping together 3.3, and Dalton Schultz contributing a sleepy 1.6. Justin Jefferson's 9.2 was the lone bright spot on a night that otherwise reads like a cry for help. Fifty point four total points is the kind of number that gets a team's name whispered next to Hall of Records entries, and not in a good way.

High Risk, High Reward 98.94, I still own you 57.38

Matt R got a huge surprise from Emmett Johnson's 22.3 points and a steady floor from Bucky Irving at 17.5, and even a modest 10.74 from Trevor Lawrence was plenty given the support. John Vaughan's night fell apart at tight end and receiver, where Brock Bowers posted a zero and DK Metcalf managed only 0.7, while Breece Hall's 17.5 was a lonely bright spot. John V also had Jacory Croskey Merritt sitting on his bench with 14.7 points unused, the kind of what if that stings extra in a series Matt R now leads 4 games to 1.

Standings and Movers

JS N My Pants sits comfortably on top at 8 and 3 after that blowout, with Lamb to the Slaughter right behind at 7 and 4, both holding steady. The real movement this week came underneath them. Hampton Water climbs a spot to 7 and 4 off the win over Matt's moms name, and High Risk, High Reward jumps two full spots to 6 and 5 behind that Emmett Johnson explosion. Will the Thrill also rides the Gibbs and Kelce show up three spots to 6 and 5. On the way down, I still own you drops three spots to 6 and 5 despite the decent record, a reminder of how fast the standings shuffle in a crowded middle. Serving Punt slides two spots to 6 and 5 as well, and F*q it we hibernate falls one spot to 5 and 6 after that bench management gaffe. As for Straight up Yorkin' it, we hold steady at 4 and 7, which I am choosing to interpret as stability rather than stagnation.

Looking Ahead

Week Twelve brings me a date with Matt's moms name, and after watching Max bench a 20 point quarterback I am feeling strangely confident. Elsewhere, Ryan gets a crack at revenge against Lamb to the Slaughter, Erica draws the unenviable task of slowing down league leading Dallas, Hampton Water and I still own you square off in a battle of comeback stories, John Sagherian tries to climb out of the basement against Matt R, and Drew Knight will be looking for anything resembling life against a hot Will the Thrill squad.

The Back Page

Look, I know how this column works. I'm a three time champion, the winningest manager in league history, and I am currently 4 and 7 with a roster that left Tre' Harris on my own bench during a six point loss. I contain multitudes. But championship pedigree doesn't evaporate, it just goes dormant, like a bear, or like Ryan's team name apparently intends. The dynasty isn't dead. It's rebuilding. Quietly. In last place. On purpose, probably. See everyone in Week Twelve.
