Week Seven: A Pretty Good Week To Be Named Matt

Let me set the scene for you. Tony Pollard dropped thirty five points on a Tuesday recap that is technically about everyone, Elijah Cohen keeps hunting former founding king glory like he never left 2016, and two separate franchises bearing the name Matt climbed the standings while the rest of the league squinted at their bench and wept. I am required by the newsletter charter I personally wrote to call this coverage unbiased. I am also required by basic human decency to tell you that Straight up Yorkin' it, my building, my dynasty, my beautiful little rowboat in a league full of yachts, won a football game this week. So did the other Matt. Let's get into it.

The Recaps

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) 102.0, Hampton Water (Austin) 92.94

I will try to be humble about this one and fail immediately. Javonte Williams went off for twenty one and a half, Josh Jacobs chipped in seventeen point six, Kyren Williams added sixteen point one, and Puka Nacua was his steady reliable self with seventeen point eight, and suddenly I am sitting here with over a hundred points looking like a genius instead of a man who has lost four games this year. Over on Austin's side, Drake Maye was an absolute monster with twenty seven point four four, but nobody told the receiving corps the memo, because Christian Watson managed only eight point two and Jaylen Waddle limped in with six point eight. The real crime scene was the bench, where Tre Tucker sat and stared at eighteen point eight points that never got to play, which would have flipped this whole result. Austin, buddy, I am sorry, but also I am the furthest thing from sorry.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) 114.02, High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) 151.52

Matt R hung a hundred and fifty one on the league and somehow it still felt earned rather than lucky, because Jaxon Smith Njigba exploded for twenty six point eight, Bucky Irving went for twenty two point five, Rhamondre Stevenson added twenty even, and Omar Cooper Jr. chipped in twenty on top of that. That is four players over twenty points on one roster, which is the fantasy equivalent of bringing a flamethrower to a knife fight. Ryan actually had a perfectly fine week himself, Caleb Williams threw for twenty two point one two and Luther Burden III popped for twenty one point seven, but Christian Kirk's donut performance and a quiet night from Kyle Pitts left too big a hole to climb out of against that kind of firepower. Sometimes you just run into a buzzsaw.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) 99.82, Will the Thrill (Tim) 78.2

Tony Pollard went for thirty five points and Tim still lost by over twenty, which tells you everything about the rest of that roster. Xavier Worthy mustered two point six, Adonai Mitchell was a complete zero, and Travis Kelce and Michael Mayer combined for barely eleven points at tight end. One elite game from one running back cannot carry a dead roster, and this week proved it in agonizing detail. Erica, meanwhile, got balanced production everywhere, Zay Flowers caught fire for twenty three point seven, Drake London added sixteen point one, and Jaylen Warren chipped in fifteen point two. The only real head scratcher on Erica's side was benching Malik Willis, who would have put up twenty six point oh two at quarterback had he started, but when you are already winning comfortably, nobody is complaining about leaving points on the table.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) 69.42, Matt's moms name (Max) 72.12

This one came down to the wire and John S has to be kicking himself over the quarterback decision, because Brock Purdy managed just twelve point oh two while Dak Prescott sat on the bench posting twenty three point two eight. Swap those two and John S wins this game comfortably. Instead Max survives thanks to Trey McBride going off for twenty two point one at tight end, even though the rest of his roster was shockingly quiet, DeVonta Smith scored zero, Nico Collins barely registered at one point six, and Jordan Addison only managed three point three. Amon-Ra St. Brown kept John S afloat with nineteen point seven, but a bad quarterback call sealed his fate.

It might be #over (Drew) 63.88, I still own you (John V) 82.56

Drew's bench is becoming a horror movie franchise at this point. Cam Skattebo sat and produced eighteen point six points of nothing while Jordan Mason started and managed six point eight, and that swap alone basically decides this game. Bo Nix had a nice day throwing for twenty five point two eight, but nobody around him showed up, Justin Jefferson was quiet with six point four and the receiving corps mostly cratered. John V got exactly what he needed from Quinshon Judkins, who erupted for twenty two point nine, even while Breece Hall contributed a flat zero. It might be #over for Drew's season hopes, frankly. The name is starting to feel less like a joke and more like a prophecy.

JS N My Pants (Dallas) 89.58, Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) 128.62

Elijah's march back toward relevance continues, and this week he did it with four different players over sixteen points, Jameson Williams with twenty three point six, CeeDee Lamb with twenty one point nine, Jonathan Taylor with eighteen, and Ashton Jeanty with seventeen point six. That is simply an avalanche. Dallas got a huge game from Joe Burrow, twenty eight point eight eight through the air, and Tee Higgins chipped in twenty three point eight, but the depth just was not there, Sam LaPorta and Isaiah Likely combined for barely four and a half points at tight end. Dallas also left nineteen point two on the bench with KC Concepcion Jr. untouched, which in a game decided by thirty nine points would not have mattered, but it is still the kind of thing that keeps a manager up at night. The history here remains brutal, Elijah now owns this series outright with Dallas still searching for his first win against him.

Standings and Movers

Sitting atop the mountain is Sampson & Dylan lila at six and one, Elijah fully in his redemption arc after that founding era championship feels like a decade ago. JS N My Pants follows at five and two despite the week seven loss, with Hampton Water right behind also at five and two. Matt's moms name and Serving Punt sit at four and three, and I still own you rounds out the four and three club. Then we hit the pack where things get interesting.

The two biggest risers this week both share my last initial, which I promise I did not rig. High Risk, High Reward jumped a spot to seventh after that one hundred fifty one point detonation, and Straight up Yorkin' it, your humble correspondent's own operation, climbed a spot to eighth after the win over Hampton Water. Meanwhile Hawk Tua on my Penix fell two spots down to ninth after that tough loss despite scoring over a hundred and fourteen points themselves, which just tells you how loaded that particular shootout was. Will the Thrill, How bout them Cowboys, and It might be #over round out the bottom with no movement, all still searching for answers.

Looking Ahead

Week eight brings me a date with It might be #over, and after watching Drew's bench produce more than his starting lineup two weeks running, I like my odds, though I say that about every matchup before kickoff and have the three and four record to show for my confidence. Elsewhere Hawk Tua gets a crack at revenge against Hampton Water, the Battle lurking John S takes on John V in another chapter of that eternal rivalry, and league leader Sampson & Dylan lila faces a dangerous Serving Punt squad that just dismantled Will the Thrill.

The Back Page

I want to be very clear that I have no personal stake in how this newsletter is written, I am simply a neutral chronicler of facts, and the facts this week happen to include me scoring over a hundred points and climbing the standings. The facts also happen to include Hampton Water leaving eighteen points of Tre Tucker on the bench, which I will be bringing up every time Austin and I play from now until the heat death of the universe. Three rings do not write themselves, folks. Somebody has to keep score, and lucky for you, that somebody also happens to be undefeated against conflicts of interest.
