Week Four: The Unbeaten Get Hunted and I Finally Admit My Bench Is a Crime Scene

Gentlemen, ladies, and the ghost of Zach Smith hovering over all of us, welcome to Week Four. We've got two unbeaten squads left standing, Sampson & Dylan Lila and Hampton Water, both sitting pretty at three and oh, and wouldn't you know it, they're not playing each other, because the schedule gods are cowards. Everybody else is bunched up in that delicious two and one, one and two soup where one Thursday night domino can flip half the standings. And yes, before you ask, my own Straight up Yorkin' it sits at two and one with a top three scoring total in the league, which I will be discussing at length below with the journalistic objectivity you have come to expect from a three time champion writing about himself.

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

This is personal. Dallas owns me in the head to head, two games to one, including a 38.22 point beatdown back in 2023 that I still think about in the shower. But I come into Thursday with Puka Nacua, Davante Adams, and Kyren Williams humming, and I'll admit my only real hesitation is Caleb Douglas starting over a certain Tyreek Hill sitting on my bench like a Lamborghini in long term parking. I get the appeal of the upside play, but Tyreek Hill is a future Hall of Famer and Caleb Douglas is, let's say, not that, yet. I'm starting to think I left him there out of superstition more than strategy. Dallas counters with Joe Burrow slinging it to a four wide monster of Tee Higgins, Tetairoa McMillan, Mike Evans, and Stefon Diggs, which is an embarrassment of riches at receiver, assuming Burrow can only pick one at a time. My pick: me, narrowly, because I refuse to let the record book say Dallas has my number three straight times while I'm the one writing the newsletter.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) vs How bout them Cowboys (John S)

Two one and two teams desperate to not be staring up at the standings from the basement. Ryan's got Dak Prescott, a receiving corps that leans on Jaxon Smith Njigba and DeVonta Smith, and a genuinely puzzling decision to bench Patrick Mahomes and A.J. Brown while starting Devaughn Vele and Luther Burden. I understand roster construction gets weird, but Mahomes sitting behind Dak Prescott is the kind of thing that gets you a strongly worded text from your league mates. John S counters with Brock Purdy and a two headed backfield of Chase Brown and D'Andre Swift, plus Amon Ra St. Brown doing Amon Ra St. Brown things. Ryan owns this series two to one with a 42.8 point blowout back in 2024, but something about leaving Mahomes on the bench makes me nervous for him. My pick: John S, with a side eye at Ryan's lineup card.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) vs Hampton Water (Austin)

The unbeaten Hampton Water rolls in at three and oh looking to make it four, but Erica has historically owned this matchup, three wins to one, including a cartoonish 68.22 point demolition in 2024. Erica is rolling with Malik Willis at quarterback, which is a bold choice against a team that just hasn't lost yet, but James Cook, Rashee Rice, and Drake London give her enough firepower to make it interesting. Austin counters with Drake Maye, a loaded backfield of Derrick Henry, Omarion Hampton, and RJ Harvey, plus George Kittle and Jaylen Waddle catching passes. That is a lot of talent for an undefeated team to be bringing to a house that has historically haunted them. My pick: Hampton Water finally breaks the curse, but I would not be shocked if Erica's history with this matchup strikes again.

Matt's moms name (Max) vs Sampson & Dylan Lila (Elijah)

Elijah's squad is the last unbeaten alongside Hampton Water, leading the league in scoring at 340.9 points, and he's getting there with Jalen Hurts, Jonathan Taylor, CeeDee Lamb, and Garrett Wilson all humming at once, a stacked lineup without a single questionable call on it. Max, at one and two, needs a signature win here and has C.J. Stroud, Bijan Robinson, and Nico Collins to work with, though I'll note Jayden Daniels sits on his bench behind C.J. Stroud, which is a fine problem to have but still worth mentioning. Max actually leads this all time series three games to two, including a 44.26 point win back in 2024, so history says upset city is possible. My pick: Elijah stays perfect, but Max has the receipts to make it uncomfortable.

Real Eaters (Drew) vs High Risk, High Reward (Matt R)

Drew has never beaten Matt R, oh and four in this series, with a 27.16 point margin back in 2023 as the widest gap. Matt R is winless at zero and three this year, so something has to give. Drew has Lamar Jackson, Justin Jefferson, and Mark Andrews rolling, a perfectly sound lineup, while Matt R counters with Trevor Lawrence, Christian McCaffrey, Ashton Jeanty, and Malik Nabers, which on paper is an absurd amount of talent for a winless team. This is the classic "good roster, bad luck" situation, and I think the touch of regression finally finds Matt R here. My pick: High Risk, High Reward finally gets one, and Drew's ghost of history keeps haunting him.

Will the Thrill (Tim) vs I still own you (John V)

Tim leads this series two games to one with a 33 point win back in 2024, and he brings Josh Allen, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Travis Kelce into Thursday looking steady as ever, a lineup with no real complaints from me. John V counters with Jordan Love throwing to Ja'Marr Chase and Matthew Golden, plus Brock Bowers at tight end, which is a strong receiving nucleus even if the running back room of Jacory Croskey Merritt and Quinshon Judkins is more handful than headliner. Both teams sit at one and two and need the win badly. My pick: Tim keeps the series lead intact, but barely.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Sampson & Dylan Lila staying unbeaten against Matt's moms name. Elijah's roster simply has no holes right now.
  • Upset special: High Risk, High Reward finally snapping their winless start against Real Eaters, powered by pure star power finally showing up on the scoreboard.
  • Closest game to call: Me against Dallas. Head to head history says bet against me, my own eyes say my roster is better, and somewhere in between is a nailbiter.

The Back Page

Bold prediction: Straight up Yorkin' it finally slays the Dallas demon, Puka Nacua goes off, and I spend next week's edition reminding everyone that the three time champion does not stay down two to one in a season series for long. Also, somewhere on my own bench, Tyreek Hill quietly outscores my starting receiver and I pretend I meant to do that.
