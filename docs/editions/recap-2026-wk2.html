By Matthew Waters

Week Two: Davante Adams Erases a Bye Week, and Everyone Else Learns to Live With Chaos

I want to be professional about this. I really do. But when your own team drops 113.98 points behind a Davante Adams game that made grown men weep into their waiver wire priority, professionalism takes a back seat to a victory lap. Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W, and yes, that's me, and yes I know how this looks) put up the kind of number that makes John V's "I still own you" trash talk age like unrefrigerated milk. Elsewhere, Elijah Cohen kept his foot on the gas atop the standings, two former bottom feeders in Hampton Water and JS N My Pants punched their way to 2 and 0, and poor Tim Gravely scored 96.72 points and still lost by 35. Week Two, ladies and gentlemen. Nobody is safe.

The Recaps

Straight up Yorkin' it 113.98, I still own you 72.2

Let me set the scene with appropriate humility: Davante Adams went off for 35.5 points and Matthew Stafford tossed in 26.98 of his own, and suddenly my bye week fears were a distant memory. Dalton Schultz chipped in a shockingly useful 20.0 from the tight end slot, and even with Quentin Johnston (1.9) and Caleb Douglas (2.9) doing their best impression of furniture, the Adams and Stafford combo buried John V early. On the other side, Ja'Marr Chase did Ja'Marr Chase things for 23.0, but Jordan Love's 12.8 and a genuinely brutal Dallas Goedert line of 0.9 sank the ship, and John V left Josh Downs (10.7) and Romeo Doubs (10.1) rotting on his bench while his lineup produced 72.2. I lead this series 4 to 1 now, and I'll allow myself one modest victory cigar before getting back to being fair to everyone else.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ 122.28, Matt's moms name 86.74

Ryan's team put on an absolute clinic behind Jaxon Smith Njigba's 38.0 and Patrick Mahomes' 28.98, a one two punch that buried Max before halftime even existed metaphorically. DeVonta Smith added 22.7 for good measure. The gut punch for Max wasn't even the final score, it was watching Dak Prescott sit on his opponent's bench for 29.76 points while Jayden Daniels managed just 13.74 in the lineup that mattered. Denzel Boston's 18.0 and a three headed backfield of Bijan Robinson, Bucky Irving, and Bhayshul Tuten kept Max respectable, but this one was a laugher from the jump. Ryan evens the all time series at 3 and 3, and he's suddenly 4 spots better than he was a week ago.

Serving Punt ✨ 81.58, Real Eaters 81.0

This one came down to fractions of a point, which feels appropriate for a game where Justin Herbert managed a putrid 4.88 and Erica still found a way to win. James Cook's 20.4 and Dalton Kincaid's 19.0 did the heavy lifting, while Aaron Jones quietly added 10.5. Drew got a strong 20.8 out of Kenneth Walker and 14.8 from Lamar Jackson, but left Mark Andrews (7.9) on the bench and got nothing from his receiving corps outside of Jalen Coker's 10.6. A three point swing either direction flips this game, and Drew will be replaying Jayden Reed's ice cold 0.9 line in his nightmares, wondering if literally anyone else in that flex spot gets it done.

Hampton Water 85.4, How bout them Cowboys 81.92

Austin's Jaxson Dart experiment produced a comical 0.8 points, and somehow Hampton Water still walked away with the win. Derrick Henry (16.2), Omarion Hampton (15.5), George Kittle (16.0) and Jaylen Waddle (17.8) all showed up to cover for the quarterback disaster, while Tre Tucker sat on the bench with a maddening 20.4 that would have won this game by itself. John Sagherian got a monster 30.7 from Amon Ra St. Brown and 12.3 from Parker Washington, but got embarrassingly little from Caleb Williams (6.72) and left Brock Purdy's 28.48 bench stashed away like a lottery ticket he forgot to scratch. The all time series is knotted at 3 and 3, but Austin's now 2 and 0 and playing with house money.

High Risk, High Reward 73.18, JS N My Pants 88.58

Matt R got a strong 27.78 from Jared Goff and 20.6 from Christian McCaffrey, but the rest of his lineup fell apart around them, with Malik Nabers managing just 0.6 points and Colston Loveland adding a symbolic 0.8. Dallas countered with a balanced attack, Joe Burrow's 16.18, Chuba Hubbard's 13.4, and Sam LaPorta's 14.2 all chipping in, while Stefon Diggs' 19.2 stayed glued to the bench and didn't even matter. Matt R now trails this series 0 to 5 all time, and at 0 and 2 he's starting to look like this year's Zach Smith, minus the great record.

Will the Thrill 96.72, Sampson & Dylan lila 131.86

Tim Gravely put up a season saving 96.72 points, got 39.82 from Josh Allen and 20.6 from Travis Kelce, and lost by 35 points anyway. That's the kind of week Elijah Cohen is having right now. CeeDee Lamb erupted for 31.3, Jonathan Taylor added 27.2, and Jalen Hurts chipped in 16.16 as Sampson & Dylan lila turned in one of the two highest scores of the week. DJ Moore actually finished at negative 0.1 for Tim, which I did not know was mathematically possible, and no amount of bench browsing (Xavier Worthy's 11.0 wasn't going to save this) fixes a game where the opponent just plain outguns you. Elijah now leads this head to head series 4 to 1 and sits alone in first place.

Standings and Movers

Sampson & Dylan lila (Elijah) sits at 2 and 0 atop the standings with 246.88 points, riding that 131.86 explosion and climbing 2 spots. Hampton Water (Austin) and JS N My Pants (Dallas) both jumped 3 spots to join him at 2 and 0, proof that close, ugly wins count exactly the same as blowouts in the standings, thank you very much. The biggest fallers were How bout them Cowboys (John S) and Real Eaters (Drew), both dropping 3 spots despite being respectable 1 and 1 teams with big point totals, a reminder that in this league scoring a lot and still losing gets you nowhere fast. Will the Thrill (Tim) also fell 3 spots to 0 and 2 despite having the third most points in the entire league, which is either deeply unlucky or a real referendum on his weekly matchup luck. As for me, Straight up Yorkin' it climbed 4 spots off the back of that Adams explosion, and I'd like the record to reflect that I am simply built differently. Hawk Tua on my Penix (Ryan) also jumped 4 spots after demolishing Max.

Looking Ahead

Week Three brings me a date with How bout them Cowboys, and John Sagherian better hope Caleb Williams shows up because I am not in a charitable mood. Elsewhere, the two unbeaten juggernauts Hampton Water and JS N My Pants collide directly, guaranteeing at least one 2 and 0 team falls back to earth, while the winless class of Will the Thrill, High Risk High Reward, and I still own you all get another shot at respectability against Max, Erica, and Ryan respectively. Buckle up.

The Back Page

I want to be clear that I did not write this newsletter simply to relive Davante Adams torching John V for 35.5 points, but here we are, and I'm not sorry about it. Three championships, the best all time win percentage in league history, and now a receiver corps that resurrected itself just in time for me to write my own headlines. Is it a conflict of interest that the man reporting the news also authored the best individual performance of the week? Absolutely. Am I going to stop? Absolutely not. See everyone next week, when I will attempt, and probably fail, to be humble about it again.
