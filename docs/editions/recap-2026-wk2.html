Week 2 Recap, 2026September 22, 2026
By Matthew Waters

Week Two Delivers a Massacre, a Near Miss, and One Very Suspicious Amount of Winning by Yours Truly

Gather close, degenerates, because Week Two had a bit of everything: a 39 point Josh Allen masterpiece that still lost, a punt joke of a matchup decided by 58 hundredths of a point, and my own Straight up Yorkin' it squad putting up a number that made John Vaughan's bench look like it was filled with traffic cones. I am, as always, contractually obligated to tell you this recap is fair and balanced. I am also the reigning three time champion writing about my own team in the same newsletter, so calibrate your trust accordingly. Let's get into it.

The Recaps

Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) 113.98, I still own you (John V) 72.2

I want to be modest about this one. I cannot be. Davante Adams went off for 35.5 points and Matthew Stafford tossed in 26.98 of his own, and suddenly I am sitting pretty at 1 and 1 with a laugher on my ledger. Dalton Schultz chipped in a very tidy 20 points because apparently my tight end room just does that now. Meanwhile John V got a totally respectable 23 points from Ja'Marr Chase and absolutely nothing else worked, with Dallas Goedert turning in a rounding error of 0.9 points at tight end. The killer for John V was the bench, where Josh Downs quietly posted 10.7 and Romeo Doubs added 10.1 that never left the sideline. I now lead this series 4 to 1, and history says the widest margin between us was 49.2 points back in 2024. We didn't touch that record this week, but I appreciate the effort toward tradition.

Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) 122.28, Matt's moms name (Max) 86.74

Ryan's Patrick Mahomes led the way with 28.98, but the real story was Jaxon Smith-Njigba going nuclear for 38 points, which is the kind of number that makes an opposing manager stare at the ceiling. Ryan also left a genuinely wild amount of points in reserve, with Dak Prescott sitting on the bench for 29.76 points he never needed. That is the definition of having too much cake. On the other side, Max got 18 points from Denzel Boston and a strong 14.1 from Trey McBride, but Jordan Addison's 3.1 points and a shallow receiver group did him in. Ryan now levels this all time series at 3 and 3, closing the gap on a rivalry that has apparently been a coin flip since day one.

Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) 81.58, Real Eaters (Drew) 81.0

If you blinked, you missed the difference. Fifty eight hundredths of a point separated these two, and honestly both managers should be a little sick to their stomachs. Erica got saved by James Cook's 20.4 and a strong 19 points from Dalton Kincaid at tight end, while Justin Herbert stumbled to just 4.88 points in the box score. Drew got 20.8 out of Kenneth Walker and 14.8 from Lamar Jackson, which on most nights is plenty, but he left Mark Andrews on the bench posting 7.9 points that would not have been enough anyway. This one is a coin flip that Erica happened to win, and the all time series now sits 3 and 2 in her favor, with the record book still showing a 48.22 point blowout from 2023 that this game was not going to threaten.

Hampton Water (Austin) 85.4, How bout them Cowboys (John S) 81.92

Austin somehow won this despite Jaxson Dart contributing a corpse like 0.8 points at quarterback, because Derrick Henry and Omarion Hampton combined for 31.7 points on the ground and George Kittle added 16 more. But let's talk about the bench, because Austin sat Tre Tucker, who dropped 20.4 points doing absolutely nothing for him. That is a lineup crime that could have blown this game up in his face. On the other side, John S got a monster 30.7 points from Amon-Ra St. Brown, but his quarterback situation was even worse than Austin's, with Caleb Williams managing only 6.72 while Brock Purdy sat on his bench racking up 28.48 points unused. Two managers, two horrific quarterback sits, and Austin escaped by 3.48 points. The rivalry is dead even at 3 and 3 in the record book, and somewhere the ghost of that 35.72 point 2023 blowout is laughing at both of these lineups.

High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) 73.18, JS N My Pants (Dallas) 88.58

Matt R got 27.78 points from Jared Goff and 20.6 from Christian McCaffrey, which sounds like a winning day, except Malik Nabers turned in a ghostly 0.6 points and Colston Loveland matched him with 0.8. When your third and fourth options no show like that, the math stops working. Dallas didn't even need a big spike game, just a well rounded attack: Chuba Hubbard's 13.4, Sam LaPorta's 14.2, and Tetairoa McMillan's 12.6 all pulled weight while Stefon Diggs sat unused for 19.2 points that Dallas fortunately did not need. Matt R now falls to 0 and 5 all time in this matchup, a truly rough historical stat line, with the record book noting a 45.22 point beatdown in 2023 for flavor.

Will the Thrill (Tim) 96.72, Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) 131.86

Josh Allen threw for a jaw dropping 39.82 points and Tim still lost by 35. Let that sink in. Travis Kelce added 20.6 and Jahmyr Gibbs chipped in 20.3, and it still was not close, because Elijah's roster simply refused to miss. Jonathan Taylor ran for 27.2, CeeDee Lamb caught his way to 31.3, and Garrett Wilson tacked on 14.2 for good measure. Tim's DJ Moore actually finished at negative 0.1 points, which is a special kind of nothing, and it capped off a night where even a career quarterback outing wasn't enough. Elijah, the fallen founding king of 2016, is looking dangerously like a man rebuilding his kingdom, and he now leads this series 4 to 1 with a 56.18 point margin on the books from 2023 that Tim would very much like to forget.

Standings and Movers

Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) sits atop the league at 2 and 0 with 246.88 points, climbing two spots after that demolition of Tim. Right behind him, Hampton Water (Austin) and JS N My Pants (Dallas) both jump three spots to join him in the unbeaten club at 2 and 0. The big fallers this week are How bout them Cowboys (John S) and Real Eaters (Drew), both dropping three spots to 1 and 1 despite solid point totals, while Will the Thrill (Tim) craters three spots to 0 and 2 despite a league high effort from Josh Allen. On the rise, Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) jumps four spots after that Smith-Njigba explosion, and I am pleased to report my own Straight up Yorkin' it climbs four spots as well, up to 8th at 1 and 1. I would like the record to reflect that I am both the author of this newsletter and a mover on the board. That is called range.

Looking Ahead

Week 3 brings me a date with How bout them Cowboys (John S), and after watching his quarterback situation implode this week, I like my chances, though I'd never say that where he can hear me. Elsewhere, Hampton Water and JS N My Pants collide in a battle of unbeaten teams that will trim the league's perfect record down to one, Real Eaters gets a brutal test against league leading Sampson & Dylan-lila, and the winless club of I still own you, High Risk High Reward, and Will the Thrill all get a chance to find the exit ramp against opponents who are not exactly murderers' row. Buckle up.

The Back Page

I want to be very clear that when I tell you Davante Adams' 35.5 points was the best story of the week, I am being a completely unbiased journalist and not a man who just watched his own team move up four spots in the standings. But look at the numbers. Look at them. I am simply a conduit for the truth, and the truth this week is that my bench outscored several entire teams and my Stafford Adams Schultz trio should be studied in football labs across the country. Everyone else, keep grinding. Some of you are winless. I am not. That is not bias, that is journalism.
