By Matthew Waters

Week Three Arrives, and the Undefeated Empire Braces for Its First Casualty

Three teams remain unbeaten heading into Thursday night, and I want you to sit with that for a second because it will not last. Sampson & Dylan lila and Hampton Water and JS N My Pants are all sitting pretty at two and zero, riding the top of a standings sheet that flatters them, but two of those three play each other this very week, which means math is about to do what math does. Meanwhile my own Straight up Yorkin it sits at one and one, a record I consider a temporary inconvenience, the kind of thing a great manager shrugs off on his way to a third ring. I have decided this is a building week. I have also decided that as the author of this newsletter I get to decide that. Let us get into it.

Straight up Yorkin it (Matt W) vs How bout them Cowboys (John S)

This series is dead even at two and two, and John S and I have been trading haymakers since the widest margin in this rivalry was a gruesome fifty eight and change back in week six of last year. I like my hand here. Puka Nacua and Davante Adams give me a receiver room John S simply cannot match snap for snap, and Kyren Williams is the kind of workhorse that wins these midweek chess matches on volume alone. John S counters with Amon Ra St. Brown and Ladd McConkey, which is a legitimately scary duo, and Chase Brown gives him bellcow work of his own. I will say this plainly because the format demands honesty even from a homer: John S benching Caleb Williams behind Brock Purdy is fine, Purdy has been steady, but stashing Rachaad White and Tyler Allgeier while starting Parker Washington and Dontayvion Wicks at receiver feels like leaving speed on the table when McConkey or St. Brown could get slowed by coverage. As for my side, I love my lineup, but I always love my lineup, which is either supreme confidence or a documented character flaw depending who you ask. Pick: Straight up Yorkin it, because a man is allowed to bet on himself in his own newsletter.

Hawk Tua on my Penix (Ryan) vs I still own you (John V)

Ryan trails this series two to three but has the far better vibes entering Thursday, sitting one and one against a John V team that is winless and looking for answers. Patrick Mahomes headlines for Ryan, and I will note he is sitting A.J. Brown on his bench, which is a luxury problem I would kill to have, though it does make me squint a little at Devaughn Vele holding a starting receiver spot over him. That is a bench Ferrari staying in the garage. John V counters with Ja'Marr Chase and Brock Bowers, a legitimately elite one two punch, and Breece Hall gives him a bruiser in the backfield, but two losses is two losses and a winless team playing outside its own comfort zone tends to press. Pick: Hawk Tua on my Penix, and Ryan should seriously consider letting Brown cook before this one kicks off.

Serving Punt (Erica) vs High Risk, High Reward (Matt R)

Erica owns this series outright, three to nothing, and Matt R has never beaten her, which is the kind of historical burden I would not wish on anyone, having personally never had to carry it. Matt R starts Christian McCaffrey and Ashton Jeanty, a genuinely stacked backfield, and Malik Nabers gives him a true alpha receiver, so the talent gap is not the story here, the story is that he is oh and two and needs this one badly. Erica rolls with Justin Herbert and a two headed backfield of James Cook and Jaylen Warren that should give her volume all game. One thing I will flag on Erica's side, Jayden Reed sitting on her bench while Jakobi Meyers holds down a starting receiver spot is a little curious given Reed's upside, but she has clearly earned the benefit of the doubt this season. Pick: Serving Punt, because history has to count for something and Matt R has not solved this puzzle yet.

Hampton Water (Austin) vs JS N My Pants (Dallas)

The marquee matchup of the week, no debate. Two unbeaten squads, series knotted at two and two, and somebody walks away from Thursday still perfect while the other learns humility. Austin brings Derrick Henry and Omarion Hampton, a two man wrecking crew in the backfield, plus George Kittle at tight end, which is about as sturdy a foundation as this league offers. Dallas counters with Joe Burrow slinging it to Tee Higgins and Tetairoa McMillan, and De'Von Achane and Saquon Barkley give him his own dynamite backfield. This is the closest thing to a title elimination game in week three and I genuinely do not know how Dallas is sitting KC Concepcion and Cooper Kupp behind Mike Evans and Chuba Hubbard, though both of those bench names carry real upside if the matchup breaks that way later in the year. Pick: Hampton Water, narrowly, because Henry in a big spot is the sort of thing that has ended a lot of undefeated dreams around this league.

Matt's moms name (Max) vs Will the Thrill (Tim)

Max leads this all time series four to nothing, the widest gap of any rivalry on the board this week, and Tim enters oh and two looking like a man in genuine danger of making it five straight. Max starts Jayden Daniels and a strong backfield tandem in Bijan Robinson and Bucky Irving, which should carry the day against a Tim roster that, on paper, actually looks loaded. Josh Allen, Jahmyr Gibbs, and Travis Kelce is a legitimate murderers row, and I have real questions about Tim starting Deebo Samuel and Adonai Mitchell over Xavier Worthy and Marvin Harrison sitting on his bench. That is a lot of talent riding the pine while a winless team searches for points. Pick: still Matt's moms name, on history and matchup fit, but if Tim ever fixes that receiver room this series gets a lot more competitive.

Real Eaters (Drew) vs Sampson and Dylan lila (Elijah)

Elijah's crew sits alone atop the standings at two and zero with the most points in the league, and the founding king of this whole operation looks like he is finally climbing out of the crater he dug for himself back in 2020 and 2021. Drew trails this particular series one to two but has the scarier ceiling on paper, with Lamar Jackson and Justin Jefferson leading the way. Elijah answers with Jalen Hurts and Jonathan Taylor, a dangerous combination, plus CeeDee Lamb keeping the receiver room honest. I do not love Drew rostering Brian Thomas on the bench while Jalen Coker and Rome Odunze hold starting spots, that feels like a lineup card that got set on autopilot. Pick: Sampson and Dylan lila, the redemption arc is real and I am not betting against the league's hottest offense right now.

Locks and Upset Watch

  • Safest pick: Sampson and Dylan lila over Real Eaters. The point differential and the record both say the same thing.
  • Upset special: JS N My Pants knocking off Hampton Water and keeping both undefeated dreams alive for exactly one more week before someone has to lose eventually.
  • Too close to call: Straight up Yorkin it versus How bout them Cowboys, and yes I am aware I am both the reporter and a participant in that sentence.

The Back Page

Bold prediction: Straight up Yorkin it climbs back to two and one this week behind a monster Puka Nacua line, the unbeaten club loses exactly one member in the Hampton Water versus JS N My Pants collision, and somewhere Elijah Cohen quietly reminds everyone that the founding king does not need a crown to still run the table. Book it, or don't, but I already have.
