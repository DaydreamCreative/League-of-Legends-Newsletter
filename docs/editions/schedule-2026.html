The 2026 Schedule Has Landed, and Somehow I Still Come Out Looking Great

Ladies, gentlemen, and the six of you who still haven't updated your team names since draft day, the 2026 schedule is here, and I have read every line of it with the careful, unbiased eye of a man who has won three championships and would very much like a fourth. Everyone plays everyone at least once this year, which means there is nowhere left to hide. The scheduling gods have also, in their infinite wisdom, given us rivalry slates in Week 1 and again on Thanksgiving, because nothing pairs better with turkey and forced family conversation than the sweet release of beating someone you already dislike on principle.

Let's start with Week 1, where the league office apparently decided subtlety was for other leagues. Straight up Yorkin' it, that's me, opens the year against Max's moms name, a Matt versus Matt situation that has been simmering since the day two grown men named their teams after the same joke. There simply is not room in this league for two Matts with that kind of confidence, and I intend to settle the matter early. Elsewhere, Good Old American Values and Matt's moms name run it back in a series so even, two and three across five meetings, that calling it a coin flip is an insult to coins. Serving Punt walks into a genuine revenge spot against KEEP POUNDING, who has owned that matchup three to one and shows no interest in being polite about it. Fitzmagic and Will the Thrill continue their strange little tug of war, one and two across three games, while Team Sagherian and Biggest Dumper renew the other great identity crisis of this league, two men, two very similar team names, one bruised ego walking away. Real Eaters and JS N My Pants round it out at two and three, because apparently every rivalry in this league has to be decided by a single game somewhere down the line.

Then we fast forward to Thanksgiving, Week 12, where most of these grudges get a rematch with better food on the table. Yorkin' it and Max's moms name run it back one more time, because once wasn't going to settle anything between two teams with identical name origins and, in my case, vastly superior rosters. Good Old American Values and Matt's moms name are still deadlocked and still refusing to fix that, Team Sagherian and Biggest Dumper get their second helping of the same identity feud, and Fitzmagic now turns to face JS N My Pants in a series so balanced, two and two, that it might as well be decided by a coin toss over the mashed potatoes. Serving Punt gets a new dance partner in Real Eaters, dead even at two apiece, and Will the Thrill finally gets a crack at revenge against KEEP POUNDING, who still holds that three to one series lead and will not let anyone forget it. Print the schedule below, mark your calendars, and start planning your excuses now. I already have mine ready, and it involves a trophy case.
