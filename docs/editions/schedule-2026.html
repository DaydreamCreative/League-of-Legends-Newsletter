The 2026 Slate Drops: Circle Your Calendars, Sharpen Your Grudges

Ladies and gentlemen, put down your mock drafts and pick up your reading glasses, because the schedule is here and it is dripping with malice. I have looked over every week of this round robin with the careful, unbiased eye of a man who has won three championships and would very much like a fourth, and I can report that the football gods have once again smiled upon my franchise. Coincidence? I report, you decide.

Week 1 wastes no time throwing gasoline on old fires. Ryan and Max run it back in a series deadlocked at two wins apiece out of five, which is the fantasy equivalent of two boxers who keep accidentally booking the same hotel. Erica gets Elijah right out of the gate in a revenge spot, since Elijah has owned that series three to one and Erica has clearly been marinating on it all offseason. Austin and Tim open a series that is basically a coin flip at one and two, and Drew opens against dallastdickinso in a five game rivalry that also sits at two and three, tight enough to keep everyone honest. And then there is the matter of the name twins. Matt W takes on Matt R in Week 1, and let the record show that this league has never been big enough for two Matts, and after this season it will only need to be big enough for the one who already has three rings. Meanwhile John S and John V settle their own identity crisis the exact same week, which means Week 1 alone has two separate existential crises on the slate before anybody has even set a lineup.

Then Thanksgiving arrives in Week 12 and the schedule makers, bless their vindictive little hearts, decided we all needed dessert with a side of vengeance. Matt W versus Matt R runs it back on turkey day, because apparently once a year of petty superiority is not enough for this league, we need two. Ryan and Max meet again too, still deadlocked, still refusing to just settle it like adults. Erica gets Drew this time in a series tied at two and two, Austin draws dallastdickinso in another dead even set at two and two, and John S versus John V returns to remind everyone that some rivalries are simply required by law twice a season. But the headliner for pure revenge theater is Tim finally getting his shot at Elijah, who still owns that series three to one, on a holiday when Tim will have nothing to do but eat, stew, and hope for the upset.

So there it is, a full round robin of grudges, rematches, and name twin standoffs, and my team sitting right in the middle of it looking, in my completely objective opinion, primed for banner number four. The full week by week schedule is printed just below. Study it, dread it, circle the dates that matter, and I will see you all back here with the receipts.
