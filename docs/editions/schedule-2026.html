The 2026 Slate Drops, and Somebody Fetch the Antacids

Gather round, degenerates, because the schedule gods have spoken and I, Matthew Waters, three time champion and the man who literally cannot stop winning this league, have been handed the parchment first. As always. Some things you just earn by being historically great, and reading the schedule before the rest of you sweat over it is one of them. The 2026 round robin is set, every manager plays every other manager at least once, and the league has once again been thoughtful enough to seed rivalry weeks in Week 1 and on Thanksgiving, presumably so I have maximum audience for my heroics.

Week 1 is a bloodbath of grudges. The name twin wars are back, with Matt W taking on Matt R in what I can only call a public service, since this league truly is not big enough for two Matts and only one of us has rings. John S and John V settle their own identity crisis the same weekend, may the better John survive the ridicule. Max finally gets a crack at Tim, who owns that series a brutal four games to nothing, so this is less a rivalry and more a hostage situation. Erica and Dallas are dead even at two apiece and looking to break the tie early, while Drew and Elijah bring a real coin flip of a series, two to one in Elijah's favor. And Austin gets to try to erase painful memories against Ryan, who has dropped three of four in that matchup, revenge games do not get more textbook than that.

Then Thanksgiving arrives and the league decides to reopen every wound at once. John V gets to defend his three to one edge over yours truly, and I would like the record to show I intend to ruin his turkey digestion personally. Matt R tries to close out Max the same way he has three times before, Tim and Erica run back their even steven series, and Dallas versus Drew is a genuine classic sitting at three and two. Elijah gets one more shot at Austin, who has embarrassingly swept that series three to nothing, and Ryan closes the holiday slate against John S in a series that is basically a coin toss with feelings. Full schedule is below, go ahead and circle your misery in advance.
