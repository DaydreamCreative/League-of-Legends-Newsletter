Week One: A Villain Falls, A Cowboy Rises, and the Villain's Team Reminds Everyone Why He Writes This Column

Well folks, the calendar flipped to a new season and the football gods decided to open with a joke at my expense, which I will now report on with the icy professional detachment you have all come to expect from me. Yes, Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) went out in Week One and posted the single worst score in the entire league, 69.6, a number so low it makes Joey Cohen's career winning percentage blush. But do not worry about my objectivity here, I am still the winningest manager in this league's history and I intend to cover this week with the same fairness I always bring, which is to say generously toward myself and skeptically toward everyone else. Elsewhere, John Sagherian dropped a legitimate legacy number, How bout them Cowboys hung 154.36 on High Risk, High Reward, and somewhere Zach Smith felt a disturbance in the force because a great record without a ring is still this league's favorite ghost story.

The Recaps

Matt's moms name (Max) 107.86, Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W) 69.6

I would love to tell you this was competitive. It was not competitive. Bijan Robinson went for 25.3, Trey McBride added 20, and Nico Collins chipped in 17.7 for Max, who now leads nothing historically since I still hold this series 3 to 1, but he sure picked a fine week to remind me that history and the present are two very different scoreboards. My guys, and I use "my" loosely because they clearly did not get the memo, gave me 21.7 from Javonte Williams and diminishing returns from there. The widest margin in this series was 49.14 back in 2024, and while this was not quite that ugly, it was ugly enough that I am declaring myself the goat of my own newsletter this week, which is either integrity or masochism.

Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah) 115.02, Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) 83.2

The fallen founding king strikes early. Elijah got a balanced attack, Jalen Hurts throwing for 24.72, Chris Olave hauling in 23.2, and Jonathan Taylor grinding out 22.6, enough firepower to run past Ryan despite David Montgomery's strong 27.4 and Jaxon Smith Njigba's 22.2. Ryan still leads this series 3 to 2, but a man chasing his first pre Sleeper ring can't love starting 0 and 1, especially with the ghost of "best team to never win" always lurking over his shoulder.

JS N My Pants (Dallas) 89.06, Serving Punt ✨ (Erica) 70.96

Erica's 2019 runner up pedigree did not show up to this one. Zay Flowers was the lone bright spot at 23.5, but Justin Herbert managed only 13.26 and the supporting cast never got going. Dallas rode Chuba Hubbard's 22.2 and got just enough from Joe Burrow and Mike Evans to escape. Erica now trails this series 2 to 3, and the widest margin on record, 53.66 back in 2024, at least says this one could have been worse.

Hampton Water (Austin) 94.92, I still own you (John V) 87.78

Derrick Henry decided he is not done yet, thundering to 34.8, and Christian Watson tacked on 29.7 as Austin, forever hunting that elusive title since his 2016 runner up finish, moved to 1 and 0. John V, the man with a ring and a basement to his name in the all time standings, got a strong 21.7 from Dallas Goedert and 18.8 from Breece Hall but came up short. Austin leads this series 3 to 1 now, nowhere near the 49.32 blowout from 2024 but a tidy win nonetheless.

How bout them Cowboys (John S) 154.36, High Risk, High Reward (Matt R) 92.94

Somebody tell John Sagherian the season just started, because 154.36 is the kind of number that shows up in the Hall of Records conversation, not Week One. Caleb Williams torched for 37.26, D'Andre Swift added a ridiculous 31.9, and Amon Ra St Brown chipped in 23.7 for good measure. Matt R got a game effort from Ashton Jeanty at 29.7 but never had a chance against that onslaught. This series is now 3 to 2 for John S, and given the standing 74.16 margin from 2025 week 4, apparently these two just don't do "close." The Battle of the Johns will have to wait, but John S is certainly making noise early.

Real Eaters (Drew) 132.36, Will the Thrill (Tim) 118.36

A shootout for the ages, and a reminder that Mr. Consistent Tim Gravely can lose a shootout too. Josh Allen went off for 34.66 and Jahmyr Gibbs added 30.1, which under normal circumstances wins you the week, except Drew's Kenneth Walker matched him blow for blow with 32.6, Justin Jefferson posted 27.2, and Lamar Jackson chipped in 23.96. Drew, who owns the lowest active win percentage among the veterans and trails this head to head 1 to 5, finally landed a haymaker in a series that has not been kind to him. The underdog bites again.

Power Rankings

  1. How bout them Cowboys (John S): Dropped 154.36 in the opener and is already speaking in Hall of Records tones.
  2. Real Eaters (Drew): Won a shootout and reminded everyone the 2022 crown was not a fluke.
  3. Sampson & Dylan-lila (Elijah): The founding king shows a pulse in year one of his redemption arc.
  4. Matt's moms name (Max): Beat me. I am contractually obligated to rank him fairly high for that alone.
  5. Hampton Water (Austin): Derrick Henry looks like he stole a time machine, Austin looks dangerous.
  6. JS N My Pants (Dallas): A quiet, professional win. Boring is a compliment in Week One.
  7. Will the Thrill (Tim): Lost with 118.36. Unlucky, not bad. Still lurking as always.
  8. High Risk, High Reward (Matt R): Jeanty gave him hope, the Cowboys took it away violently.
  9. I still own you (John V): The 2020 champ reminds us a ring does not stop a Week One loss.
  10. Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan): Big name talent, small week result. The "never wins it" file grows.
  11. Serving Punt ✨ (Erica): A rough start for a former runner up. The record book remembers better days.
  12. Straight up Yorkin' it (Matt W): Yes, that's me, last place, 69.6 points. The dynasty will regroup. It always does.

Looking Ahead

Week Two brings me a get right game against I still own you (John V), two 0 and 1 teams desperate to avoid an 0 and 2 hole, and I fully expect to win that one because this newsletter needs a hero arc. But the marquee matchup is Hampton Water (Austin) against How bout them Cowboys (John S), a battle of the league's only two teams still floating above 94 points, undefeated versus undefeated. Also keep an eye on Hawk Tua on my Penix ✝️ (Ryan) hosting Matt's moms name (Max), because Ryan cannot afford to start 0 and 2 with that "best team to never win" reputation already following him around.
